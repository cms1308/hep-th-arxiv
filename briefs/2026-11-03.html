The Devil Went Down to de Sitter: Divergent Effective Field Theory Expansions in Cosmology

arXiv:2611.00189 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Daniele Dorigoni , Laura Engelbrecht , Nadine Nussbaumer , Guilherme L. Pimentel
한국어

de Sitter space 및 더 일반적으로 가속하는 cosmology에서의 effective field theory (EFT)는 flat space에는 없는 현상들, 특히 spontaneous particle production에 의해 생성되는 long-range correlation을 포착해야 한다. 이러한 시간에 의존하는 배경에서 heavy physics를 integrate out할 때, 이러한 효과는 EFT expansion에 흔적을 남긴다: tree level에서조차, 이는 일반적으로 convergent series가 아니라 asymptotic series가 된다. 이것은 EFT의 실패가 아니다. expansion coefficient들의 large-order behavior는 어떤 유한한 order에서도 접근할 수 없는 물리적 정보를 암호화하고 있으며, 따라서 cosmological EFT들은 숨겨진 데이터를 담고 있다. 우리는 resurgence analysis를 이용하여 이 정보가 perturbative expansion으로부터 exact하고 non-perturbative한 예측을 복원하기 위해 어떻게 체계적으로 추출될 수 있는지를 보인다. 우리가 연구하는 예시에서, resurgent completion은 자연스럽게 de Sitter $S$-matrix의 관점으로 구성된다. 이는 우리로 하여금 일반적으로 cosmological EFT로부터 $S$-matrix 데이터를 추출하기 위한 처방을 추측하도록 이끈다. 이 framework는 particle production이나 유사한 non-perturbative 현상이 EFT expansion을 asymptotic하게 만드는 모든 상황으로 확장된다. 더 상세하고 교육적인 다룸은 더 긴 동반 논문에서 제공될 것이다.

Abstract

Effective field theories (EFTs) in de Sitter space, and in accelerating cosmologies more generally, must capture phenomena absent in flat space, most notably long-range correlations generated by spontaneous particle production. When heavy physics is integrated out in such time-dependent backgrounds, these effects leave an imprint on the EFT expansion: even at tree level, this is generically an asymptotic rather than convergent series. This is not a failure of EFT. The large-order behavior of the expansion coefficients encodes physical information inaccessible at any finite order, so cosmological EFTs contain hidden data. Using resurgence analysis, we show how this information can be systematically extracted to recover exact and non-perturbative predictions from the perturbative expansion. In the example we study, the resurgent completion is naturally organized in terms of the de Sitter $S$-matrix. This leads us to conjecture a prescription for extracting $S$-matrix data from cosmological EFTs in general. The framework extends to any setting where particle production, or a similar non-perturbative phenomenon, renders the EFT expansion asymptotic. A longer companion paper will provide a more detailed and pedagogical treatment.

Non-Invertible Charges for M-Theory

arXiv:2611.00196 PDF hep-th
Kenneth Intriligator , Aiden Sheckler , Eric Y. Yang
한국어

M-theory M2 brane와 M5 brane의 gauge charge들은 거의 6-group의 charge들을 닮아 있다. 그러한 6-group의 charge들은 non-invertible fusion을 갖는 연산자들로 모형화될 것으로 예상된다. 우리는 여기서 연속적인 2-group과 6-group에 대한 non-invertible charge defect들을 고려하여, M2 brane과 M5 brane의 gauge charge를 포착하고자 한다. 우리는 "pseudo 6-group" $\frak{G}$의 charge에 대한 모형을 발견하였는데, 이는 M2 brane 및 M5 brane의 알려진 특성들과 놀라운 유사성을 보인다. 여기에는 Hanany-Witten move, $\frak{a}_{N-1}$ charge, higher-form symmetry, M5-brane worldvolume theory의 singleton decomposition, 그리고 M5/anti-M5 state들의 coupling이 포함된다.

Abstract

The gauge charges of M-theory M2 and M5 branes almost resemble the charges of a 6-group. The charges of such a 6-group are expected to be modeled by operators with non-invertible fusion. We here consider non-invertible charge defects for continuous 2-groups and 6-groups, aiming to capture the gauge charges of M2 and M5 branes. We find a model for charges of a "pseudo 6-group" $\frak{G}$ which possesses remarkable similarities with known features of M2 and M5 branes, including: Hanany-Witten moves, the $\frak{a}_{N-1}$ charges, higher-form symmetry, and singleton decomposition of M5-brane worldvolume theories, and couplings of M5/anti-M5 states.

The Multi-trace Eigenstate Thermalization Hypothesis

arXiv:2611.00197 PDF hep-thcond-mat.stat-mech
Ahmed I. Abdalla , Gabriele Di Ubaldo , Elisa Tabor
한국어

우리는 multi-trace eigenstate thermalization hypothesis를 소개하는데, 이는 ETH를 확장하여 여러 개의 분리된 index cycle을 포함한 임의의 index 구조를 갖는 operator matrix element들의 connected correlation을 다루는 것이다. 우리는 local rotational invariance로부터 multi-trace ETH를 유도하고, 그 독립적인 데이터를 higher-order free cumulant와 연관시키며, 그 결과로 생기는 multi-cycle 데이터를 암호화하는 cyclic matrix model을 구성한다. 우리는 chaotic quantum system에서 multi-trace ETH에 대한 근거를 제시하고 low-dimensional gravity에서 multi-trace correlation을 계산한다. few-body system에서는, one-point spectral form factor에 대한 linear ramp contribution을 포함하여, multi-trace contribution이 correlated periodic orbit로부터 발생한다. 2차원에서는, JT gravity에서 quartically coupled bulk scalar로부터 발생하는 interaction-induced multi-trace correlation을 계산하며, winding matter diagram을 matter-channel weight 및 anomalous dimension의 보정과 연관시킨다. 3차원에서는, multi-trace non-Gaussianity가 topology-changing saddle과 light matter 모두로부터 발생함을 보인다. Virasoro TQFT와 conformal mean-field theory를 이용하여, knot 및 link complement, 그리고 matter loop을 갖는 torus wormhole을 포함하는 예시들을 계산한다. 이러한 모든 상황에 걸쳐, multi-trace ETH는 chaotic quantum system에서의 connected correlator와 gravity에서의 multi-boundary wormhole amplitude 모두를 위한 공통의 framework를 제공한다.

Abstract

We introduce a multi-trace eigenstate thermalization hypothesis, an extension of ETH that governs connected correlations of operator matrix elements with arbitrary index structures, including several disjoint index cycles. We derive multi-trace ETH from local rotational invariance, relate its independent data to higher-order free cumulants, and construct a cyclic matrix model that encodes the resulting multi-cycle data. We provide evidence for multi-trace ETH in chaotic quantum systems and compute multi-trace correlations in low-dimensional gravity. In few-body systems, multi-trace contributions arise from correlated periodic orbits, including a linear ramp contribution to the one-point spectral form factor. In two dimensions, we compute interaction-induced multi-trace correlations from a quartically coupled bulk scalar in JT gravity, relating winding matter diagrams to corrections of matter-channel weights and anomalous dimensions. In three dimensions, we show that multi-trace non-Gaussianities arise from both topology-changing saddles and light matter. Using Virasoro TQFT and conformal mean-field theory, we compute examples involving knot and link complements and torus wormholes with matter loops. Across these settings, multi-trace ETH provides a common framework for both connected correlators in chaotic quantum systems and multi-boundary wormhole amplitudes in gravity.

Abstract

We give a physical interpretation of the deformation parameters, in the case of one or several 7-branes, and deduce behavior under Hanany-Witten transitions and changes of tesselation.

An obstruction to generalised discrete torsion in string theory

arXiv:2611.00209 PDF hep-th
Carlo Angelantonj , Giorgio Leone , Diego Perugini
한국어

우리는 orbifold compactification에서 generalised discrete torsion을 켜는 가능성을 재고하는데, 이는 주어진 twisted sector의 서로 다른 fixed point들에 서로 다른 discrete torsion phase를 부여하는 것이다. $T^6/\mathbb{Z}_2^2$ 및 $T^7/\mathbb{Z}_2^3$의 경우에 대한 genus one 및 genus two vacuum amplitude들을 명시적으로 조사함으로써, 우리는 이것이 String Theory에서는 불가능함을 보인다. 실제로, 이 generalised discrete torsion은 genus one에서는 modular invariant이지만, spectrum에 대한 비일관적인 해석으로 이어지며, 이는 다시 genus-two Riemann surface에서의 modular invariance의 상실을 함의한다. 우리의 발견은 equivariant cohomology에서 허용된 topological deformation들에 대한 최근의 분류와 일치하는데, 이 분류는 이러한 가능성을 포함하지 않는다.

Abstract

We reconsider the possibility of turning on generalised discrete torsion in orbifold compactifications, whereby different discrete torsion phases are attached to different fixed points of a given twisted sector. By an explicit inspection of the genus one and genus two vacuum amplitudes for the $T^6/\mathbb{Z}_2^2$ and $T^7/\mathbb{Z}_2^3$ cases, we show that this is not possible in String Theory. In fact, this generalised discrete torsion, although modular invariant at genus one, leads to an inconsistent interpretation of the spectrum which, in turn, implies a loss of modular invariance on genus-two Riemann surfaces. Our finding is in agreement with a recent classification of allowed topological deformations in equivariant cohomology, which does not include this possibility.

The pinning-field line defect at finite temperature

arXiv:2611.00210 PDF hep-th
Julien Barrat , Simone Giombi
한국어

우리는 critical $\mathrm{O}(N)$ model에서 유한 온도에서의 pinning-field line defect를 연구하며, 이는 quantum critical point에서의 정적인 symmetry-breaking impurity를 서술한다. operator product expansion (OPE), analyticity, 그리고 thermal periodicity를 이용하여, 우리는 heavy-operator asymptotics를 low-lying thermal OPE 데이터와 연관시키는 명백히 periodic한 expansion을 구성한다. 우리는 $\varepsilon$-expansion에서 thermal correlator와 one-point coefficient를 결정하고, 이 결과들을 two-dimensional Ising endpoint와 결합하여, three-dimensional Ising model에서 가장 낮은 defect operator에 대한 Padé 추정값 $\langle \hat{\phi} \rangle \sim 1.701/\beta^{\smash{\hat{\Delta}_{\smash{\hat{\phi}}}}}$ 를 얻는다. 우리는 periodic OPE를 몇 개의 low-lying operator로 truncation하는 것이 defect correlator를 정확하게 근사한다고 추측한다. 이를 검증하기 위해, 우리는 large-$N$ thermal saddle을 수치적으로 풀어 $2<d<4$ 전체에 걸쳐 defect one-point coefficient를 추출한다. 3차원에서는, tilt 및 longitudinal singlet two-point function을 계산하여, identity와 가장 낮은 singlet으로 truncation된 periodic OPE와 매우 좋은 일치를 발견한다. 마지막으로, 우리는 sphere 위의 antipodal defect를 이용하여 유한한 spatial volume으로 분석을 확장한다.

Abstract

We study the pinning-field line defect in the critical $\mathrm{O}(N)$ model at finite temperature, describing a static symmetry-breaking impurity at a quantum critical point. Using the operator product expansion (OPE), analyticity, and thermal periodicity, we construct a manifestly periodic expansion relating heavy-operator asymptotics to low-lying thermal OPE data. We determine thermal correlators and one-point coefficients in the $\varepsilon$-expansion and, combining these results with the two-dimensional Ising endpoint, obtain the Padé estimate $\langle \hat{\phi} \rangle \sim 1.701/\beta^{\smash{\hat{\Delta}_{\smash{\hat{\phi}}}}}$ for the lowest defect operator in the three-dimensional Ising model. We conjecture that truncating the periodic OPE to a few low-lying operators accurately approximates defect correlators. To test this, we numerically solve the large-$N$ thermal saddle and extract defect one-point coefficients across $2<d<4$. In three dimensions, we compute the tilt and longitudinal singlet two-point functions, finding very good agreement with the periodic OPE truncated to the identity and lowest singlet. Finally, we extend the analysis to finite spatial volume using antipodal defects on the sphere.

A local action for Kodaira--Spencer gravity

arXiv:2611.00213 PDF hep-thgr-qcmath-ph
Olaf Hohm
한국어

Calabi--Yau threefold의 복소구조 변형(complex structure deformation)에 대한 제약을 새로운 gravity field로서의 $(1,1)$ form을 이용해 풀어냄으로써 Kodaira--Spencer gravity에 대한 local action을 제시한다. gauge algebra는 Courant algebroid에 기반한 진정한 $L_{\infty}$ algebra가 되며, gravity field와 그 선형화된 gauge 변환은 double field theory의 그것과 일치한다. 실삼차원 slice 위에서 free action은 massless spin-2에 대한 Fierz--Pauli action과 B-field에 대한 Kalb--Ramond action으로 환원된다. 비선형적으로는 이 이론이 세 개의 미분을 갖는 parity-odd interaction을 포함하며, level-matching 제약 없이 gauge invariant하므로, 이러한 의미에서 unconstrained double field theory를 정의한다. 전체 이론은 cyclic $L_{\infty}$ algebra로 encoding되며, 이에 대한 double copy 해석이 주어진다.

Abstract

A local action is given for Kodaira--Spencer gravity by solving a constraint on the complex structure deformation of a Calabi--Yau threefold in terms of a $(1,1)$ form as the new gravity field. The gauge algebra becomes a genuine $L_{\infty}$ algebra based on a Courant algebroid, while the gravity field and its linearized gauge transformations coincide with those of double field theory. On a real three-dimensional slice, the free action reduces to the Fierz--Pauli action for massless spin-2 plus the Kalb--Ramond action for a B-field. Non-linearly, the theory carries parity-odd interactions with three derivatives, and it is gauge invariant without a level-matching constraint, in this sense defining an unconstrained double field theory. The full theory is encoded in a cyclic $L_{\infty}$ algebra, for which a double copy interpretation is given.

Reconstructing string dualities through symmetries of moduli spaces

arXiv:2611.00235 PDF hep-th

한국어

우리는 quantum gravity moduli space의 asymptotic region과 관련된 perturbative regime을 특징짓는 가능한 duality 기술들에 대해 bottom-up 분석을 수행한다. 우리의 접근법은 scalar manifold로서의 이러한 moduli space들의 symmetry와, 가벼워지는 states의 가능한 tower를 제약하여 무한거리에서 나타나는 dual theory들의 해석을 제한하는 Emergent String Conjecture에 의존한다. 32개와 16개의 supercharge를 갖는 supergravity 이론에 대해, 우리의 재구성은 각각 M-theory/Type II string theory와 heterotic string theory의 top-down toroidal compactification과 연관된 duality frame들과 유일하게 일치한다. supersymmetry가 더 적은 이론의 경우 moduli space는 더 적은 symmetry를 나타내며, quantum correction이 classical moduli space의 특정 방향을 접근 불가능하게 만들 수 있다. 그럼에도 불구하고 우리는 여전히 가능한 duality 배열들을 제약할 수 있다. 이들은 대부분 알려진 top-down construction에 의해 실현되지만, string landscape의 아직 탐구되지 않은 영역에 놓여 있을 수 있는 흥미로운 추가 가능성들도 존재한다.

Abstract

We perform a bottom-up analysis of the possible duality descriptions characterizing the perturbative regimes associated to the asymptotic regions of quantum gravity moduli spaces. Our approach relies on the symmetries of such moduli spaces as scalar manifolds, as well as the Emergent String Conjecture, which constrains the possible towers of states becoming light, and thus the interpretation of the dual theories emerging at infinite distance. For supergravity theories with 32 and 16 supercharges, our reconstruction uniquely matches those duality frames associated to top-down toroidal compactifications of M-theory/Type II string theory and heterotic string theory, respectively. For theories with less supersymmetry, moduli spaces exhibit fewer symmetries, and quantum corrections can render certain directions of the classical moduli space inaccessible; nonetheless, we are still able to constrain the possible arrangements of dualities. While these are mostly realized by known top-down constructions, there are some intriguing additional possibilities which might lay in unexplored corners of the string landscape.

Abstract

Five-dimensional superconformal field theories are intrinsically strongly coupled fixed points that can be realized as M-theory on local Calabi-Yau threefolds with canonical singularities. The low-energy effective theory on their extended Coulomb branch is governed by a cubic prepotential, determined by the triple intersection numbers of divisors in the resolved geometry. For toric threefolds, dual to 5-brane webs, these numbers are computed combinatorially from the toric polygon. However, no such description has been available for generalized toric polygons (GTPs), dual to 5-brane webs with 7-branes, since the corresponding generalized toric Calabi-Yau threefolds are generally non-toric. We show that the 7-brane data are encoded by additional lines in the GTP, which correspond to non-toric, non-compact divisors that we call exuberant divisors. Building on a local analysis of these divisors and on the associativity of the intersection product, we develop an algorithm that computes the triple intersection numbers, and hence the prepotential, directly from the combinatorial data of the GTP. We illustrate it in examples and provide a Mathematica code implementation of the intersection algorithm.

Abstract

We then let $e^2$ itself flow. Owing to the manifest gauge invariance of the GFERG, the flow of~$e^2$ is governed solely by the photon anomalous dimension~$\gamma_A$, which remains positive along both branches up to the merging point. Hence, no fixed point with~$e^2\neq0$ exists. Flows from a sufficiently large~$e^2$ in the ultraviolet (UV) reach the chirally broken phase even for vanishing four-Fermi couplings, so the gauge interaction does trigger chiral symmetry breaking. The merging point, however, is not a fixed point, and the gauge coupling vanishes in any continuum limit within our truncation.
