Isomorphisms of Class S Theories with IndexLab

arXiv:2611.21711 PDF hep-th
Zhihao Duan , Zhenghao Zhong
한국어

우리는 IndexLab을 사용하여 rank 7 이하의 ADE Lie algebra는 물론 $A_8$과 $E_8$에 기반한 untwisted 및 twisted class $\mathcal{S}$ 구성들을 탐색한다. 최대 5개의 puncture를 가진 genus 3 이하의 surface를 고려하며, 총 167,244,647개의 구성을 다룬다. 동형인 이론의 후보 쌍을 찾기 위해 Schur 및 Hall-Littlewood index, Weyl anomaly, Coulomb branch spectrum, 그리고 flavor data를 비교한다. 알려진 메커니즘으로 설명되는 쌍들을 제외하면 5,278개의 후보 쌍이 남는다. rank, puncture label, twist의 반복적인 변화는 동형 후보들의 family를 시사한다. 우리는 쌍의 99.58%를 25개의 family와 6개의 finite family로 분류한다. Abstract

We use IndexLab to scan untwisted and twisted class $\mathcal{S}$ constructions based on ADE Lie algebras of rank up to seven, as well as $A_8$ and $E_8$. We consider surfaces of genus at most three with up to five punctures, covering 167,244,647 constructions in total. To find candidate pairs of isomorphic theories, we compare Schur and Hall-Littlewood indices, Weyl anomalies, Coulomb branch spectra, and flavor data. We set aside pairs explained by known mechanisms, leaving a catalogue of 5,278 candidate pairs. Recurring changes in rank, puncture labels, and twists suggest families of candidate isomorphisms. We group 99.58% of the pairs into 25 families and 6 finite families. IndexLab is available at this http URL .

An infrared-safe $S$-matrix for $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills

arXiv:2611.21713 PDF hep-th
Maria Nocchi , Leonardo Rastelli
한국어

무질량 gauge theory의 scattering amplitude는 infrared divergence를 가지며 표준적인 regulator가 perturbation theory 또는 planar limit에 의존하기 때문에 observable이 아니다. 우리는 Dirichlet 경계조건을 가진 rigid AdS$_4$ 위의 $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills 이론을 연구하며, 여기서는 amplitude의 역할을 boundary operator의 correlation function이 맡는다. 이 correlation function은 유한하고, 임의의 coupling과 임의의 gauge group에 대해 정의된다. 이 중 flavor-current supermultiplet의 four-point function은 기본 supergluon 상태의 $2\to2$ amplitude에 대응하며, 이 이론에서 가장 단순한 non-trivial observable이라 할 수 있다. 본 논문에서는 이를 weak coupling에서 one loop까지 계산한다. tree-level correlator는 microscopic theory로부터 직접 얻어지며, AdS unitarity로부터 구한 one-loop correlator는 Mellin space에서 놀랍도록 단순한 형태를 띤다. flat-space limit에서 이는 one-loop scattering amplitude를 재현하며, infrared divergence는 logarithm으로 다시 나타난다.

Abstract

Scattering amplitudes of massless gauge theories are not observables: they are infrared divergent, and the standard regulators are tied either to perturbation theory or the planar limit. We study $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills theory on rigid AdS$_4$ with Dirichlet boundary conditions, where the role of the amplitude is played by correlation functions of boundary operators. These are finite, and they are defined at any coupling and for any gauge group. Among them, the four-point function of the flavor-current supermultiplet is the counterpart of the $2\to2$ amplitude of the elementary supergluon states, and arguably the simplest non-trivial observable of the theory. In this paper, we compute it at weak coupling to one loop. The tree-level correlator follows directly from the microscopic theory, and the one-loop correlator, which we obtain from AdS unitarity, takes a remarkably simple form in Mellin space. In the flat-space limit, it reproduces the one-loop scattering amplitude, with the infrared divergences re-emerging as logarithms.

'Forward-backward' flows and integration cycles for semiclassical Euclidean gravity

arXiv:2611.21717 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Nicholas M. K. Ross , Toby Wiseman
한국어

Euclidean semiclassical gravity를 실수 Riemannian metric에 대해 적분하는 것은 conformal factor problem 때문에 미묘하다. Gibbons-Hawking-Perry (GHP)는 conformal perturbation에 대한 적분 contour를 허수 방향으로 회전시키는 perturbative 처방을 제시하였다. 잠재적인 non-perturbative 해결책은 semiclassical saddle point에 연관된 integration cycle을 Picard-Lefschetz theory의 의미에서 complex metric의 공간으로 변형하는 것이다. 여기서 우리는 이러한 complex integration cycle의 non-perturbative 구성을 제안한다. 이는 saddle point에서 출발하는 Einstein-Hilbert action의 'forward-backward' gradient flow를 사용하며, 이 flow를 따라 action의 실수부는 단조 증가하고 허수부는 일정하다. 이 flow는 parabolic하지 않다. saddle point 근방에서 conformal factor의 실수부는 앞으로, conformal class의 실수부는 뒤로 flow하며, 허수부에 대해서는 그 반대이다. saddle point에서 flow를 시작할 때, 대략적으로 말해 flow의 끝에서 conformal class의 실수부와 conformal factor의 허수부를 지정하게 된다. flow를 풀면 해당 saddle의 integration cycle 내부의 1-parameter 경로를 따른 complex metric을 얻으며, 이 최종 data를 변화시키면 integration cycle, 즉 saddle point의 Lefschetz thimble이 생성된다. 실수 saddle point 근방에서 이것이 GHP 처방을 재현함을 보인다. 또한 이러한 forward-backward flow가 존재할 수 있는 saddle 근방을 규명하며, Kontsevich-Segal-Witten의 조건보다 약한 제한을 얻는다. 우리는 4차원 gravity에 대해 flow를 수치적으로 명시적으로 구현하여, Euclidean de Sitter와 cavity 안의 Schwarzschild로 주어지는 saddle의 integration contour를 가로지르는 단면을 구성한다.

Abstract

Integrating Euclidean semiclassical gravity over real Riemannian metrics is subtle due to the conformal factor problem. A perturbative prescription was given by Gibbons-Hawking-Perry (GHP) where the integration contour for the conformal perturbations is rotated to be imaginary. A potential non-perturbative remedy is that the integration cycles associated to semiclassical saddle points should be deformed into the space of complex metrics in the sense of Picard-Lefschetz theory. Here we propose a non-perturbative construction of such complex integration cycles. This employs a 'forward-backward' gradient flow of the Einstein-Hilbert action, starting at a saddle point, along which the action has monotonically increasing real part and constant imaginary part. The flow is not parabolic. In a neighbourhood of a saddle point, the real part of the conformal factor flows forward and that of the conformal class backwards, with the opposite true of their imaginary parts. Starting the flow at a saddle point, then roughly speaking, one specifies the real part of the conformal class and the imaginary part of the conformal factor at the end of the flow. Solving the flow one finds the complex metrics along a one parameter path within the integration cycle of that saddle, and varying this final data generates the integration cycle, the Lefschetz thimble of the saddle point. We find that near to a real saddle point this reproduces the GHP prescription. We characterise the neighbourhood of a saddle where such forward-backward flows may exist, finding a weaker restriction than that of Kontsevich-Segal-Witten. We numerically demonstrate flows explicitly for 4-dimensional gravity, constructing sections through the integration contours for the saddles given by Euclidean de Sitter, and for Schwarzschild in a cavity.

Cosmic Censorship in the Landscape of SUSY-Broken AdS Vacua

arXiv:2611.21722 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Netta Engelhardt , Richard Nally
한국어

우리는 IIA 및 IIB string theory의 non-supersymmetric vacua에서 weak cosmic censorship의 징후를 조사한다. 특히 Ryu-Takayanagi를 이용해 holographic하게 증명될 수 있고, 구대칭 initial data에 대해 SUSY AdS vacua에서 독립적으로 증명될 수 있는 Penrose Inequality를 살펴본다. 우리는 "weak fake superpotential"이라 부르는 함수가 존재한다면, string compactification의 경우를 포함하여 AdS에서 gravity와 결합된 scalar의 임의의 EFT가 Penrose Inequality와 positive mass theorem을 모두 만족함을 증명한다. 이 조건이 IIA의 DGKT skew-whiffed vacua에 대해 전역적으로 만족됨을 보인다. 이 DGKT vacuum에서는 알려진 nonperturbative instability가 없으므로, 이는 IIA SUSY-broken AdS vacua가 절대적으로 안정할 수 있다는 증거를 제공한다. 또한 IIB의 KKLT construction에 있는 일부 SUSY-broken AdS vacua에서 weak fake superpotential이 field space에서 국소적으로 존재함을 발견한다. 따라서 여기서 고려한 틀 안에서 일부 false SUSY-broken KKLT AdS vacua는 vacuum decay에 대해 안정하다. 반대로 일부 KKLT vacua는 weak fake superpotential을 갖지 않으며, 이에 상응하여 Penrose Inequality를 위반하고 positive mass theorem이 성립하지 않는다. DGKT vacua는 designer gravity boundary condition이 있을 때에만 Penrose Inequality를 위반한다. 연구한 모든 경우에서 이는 false vacuum decay 때문이다. 우리는 이러한 SUSY-broken AdS vacua에서 이 위반이 holography와 black hole thermodynamics에 갖는 함의를 논의한다.

Abstract

We investigate signatures of weak cosmic censorship in non-supersymmetric vacua of both IIA and IIB string theory. In particular, we examine the Penrose Inequality, which can be proven holographically using Ryu-Takayanagi, and can be independently proven in SUSY AdS vacua for spherically symmetric initial data. We prove that any EFT of scalars coupled to gravity in AdS, including in particular those of string compactifications, satisfies both the Penrose Inequality and a positive mass theorem if a function that we call a "weak fake superpotential" exists. We demonstrate that this condition is satisfied globally for the DGKT skew-whiffed vacua in IIA. Since there is no known nonperturbative instability in this DGKT vacuum, this provides evidence that the IIA SUSY-broken AdS vacua may be absolutely stable. We also find the weak fake superpotential exists locally in field space in some SUSY-broken AdS vacua in a KKLT construction in IIB. Thus, within the framework considered here, some false SUSY-broken KKLT AdS vacua are stable against vacuum decay. Conversely, we find that some KKLT vacua do not have a weak fake superpotential, and correspondingly violate the Penrose Inequality and fail to have a positive mass theorem; the DGKT vacua only violate the Penrose Inequality in the presence of designer gravity boundary conditions. In all cases studied, this is due to false vacuum decay. We discuss the implications of these violations for holography and black hole thermodynamics in these SUSY-broken AdS vacua.

Abstract

Spectral determinants encode the meson spectra of planar two-dimensional QCD and of Ising field theory in the two-particle Bethe-Salpeter approximation. We derive localization identities for these determinants which express their logarithmic derivatives with respect to the spectral parameter in terms of Baxter $Q-$functions evaluated at special points, proving previously conjectured relations. Our derivation uses finite-rank commutation relations and virial identities of the underlying integral spectral operators. We identify a sufficient rational closure condition on the symbol of these operators that extends localization to a broader class of spectral problems and determines the evaluation points. Applications include generalized two-dimensional QCD with a quartic gauge-field potential and symbols interpolating between the QCD and Ising cases. We also give a general construction of the spectral operators and establish conditions for the finite-rank reduction on which localization relies.
