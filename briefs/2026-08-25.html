String stars, small black holes, and the supersymmetric index

arXiv:2608.21511 PDF hep-th
Erez Y. Urbach
한국어

우리는 각속도가 supersymmetric index $\beta \Omega = 2\pi i$에 접근할 때 black hole-string transition의 여러 측면을 논의한다. 이 극한에서 고온의 Atick-Witten 유효 기술은 붕괴한다. Heterotic string theory의 경우, index 근방에서 자기장 프로파일을 갖는 Higgsed $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_R$ supergravity의 관점에서 새로운 고온 기술이 나타난다. Hagedorn instability는 배경 자기장으로 인한 Ambjorn-Olesen W boson condensation의 형태로 이 이론에서 나타난다. type II의 경우, index 근방에서 winding string의 tower가 가벼워지며, 이는 T-dual picture에서 새로운 차원을 열어준다. 두 경우 모두에서 Horowitz-Polchinski solution과 유사한 string star solution이 발견되지만, index $\beta \Omega = 2\pi i$에서 decouple된다. charged solution을 고려하면, string star는 charged black hole과 free string phase 사이를 보간하는 것으로 보인다. index에서는 charged black hole과 charged string star phase가 모두 decouple된다. 이는 매끄러운 black hole-string transition과 stringy BPS two-charge black hole의 존재 사이에 긴장 관계가 있음을 시사한다.

Abstract

We discuss aspects of the black hole-string transition as the angular velocity approaches the supersymmetric index $\beta \Omega = 2\pi i$. In this limit, the high-temperature Atick-Witten effective description breaks down. For Heterotic string theory, a new high-temperature description emerges near the index in terms of a Higgsed $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_R$ supergravity with a magnetic field profile. The Hagedorn instability manifests in the theory as Ambjorn-Olesen W boson condensation, due to the background magnetic field. For type II, a tower of winding strings becomes light near the index, which opens up a new dimension in the T-dual picture. In both cases, a string star solution is found, similar to the Horowitz-Polchinski solution, but decouples at the index $\beta \Omega = 2\pi i$. Considering charged solutions, the string star appears to interpolate between charged black holes and the free string phase. At the index, both the charged black hole and the charged string star phases decouple. This suggests a tension between a smooth black hole-string transition and the existence of a stringy BPS two-charge black hole.

On flat space limits of $AdS_4$ black holes

arXiv:2608.21518 PDF hep-th
Pedro Vicente Marto , Shradha Ramakrishnan , Stefan Vandoren
한국어

flat space holography를 이해하고자 하는 관점에서, 우리는 gauged $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity에서 asymptotically $AdS_4$ black hole의 flat space limit을 연구한다. holographic renormalisation을 이용하여, 우리는 이 설정에서 다양한 black hole의 on-shell action의 flat space limit을 세밀하게 분석하고, 이 극한에서 partition function에 기여하는 state들을 명시적으로 제시한다. 우리는 몇 가지 특징을 발견한다: 큰 black hole은 partition function의 발산을 야기하며, 이는 thermodynamic volume divergence로 이해될 수 있다. 한편 semiclassical 수준에서, 작은 black hole은 그에 대응하는 flat space 대응물의 partition function을 재현하며, 우리는 charged 및 rotating solution 양쪽에서 BPS, (near-)extremal 또는 non-extremal black hole 사이의 매핑을 명확히 한다. 마지막으로, M-theory에서 BPS Kerr-Newman-$AdS_4$ black hole의 적절한 flat limit을 연구함으로써, 우리는 flat space에서 charged BPS solution을 얻는다. dual side에서, 우리는 large $N$에서 $S^1\times S^2$ 위의 알려진 ABJM partition function에 대한 Carrollian-like limit을 결정하고, 이것이 asymptotically flat space에서 charged BPS black hole의 올바른 entropy를 재현함을 보인다.

Abstract

With a view towards understanding flat space holography, we study the flat space limit of asymptotically $AdS_4$ black holes in gauged $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity. Using holographic renormalisation, we carefully analyse the flat space limit of the on-shell action of various black holes in this setting and make explicit the states that contribute to the partition function in this limit. We find several features: large black holes lead to a divergence in the partition function which can be understood as a thermodynamic volume divergence. Meanwhile, semiclassically, small black holes reproduce the partition function of their flat space counterparts, and we clarify the mapping between BPS, (near-)extremal or non-extremal black holes on both sides for charged and rotating solutions. Finally, by studying a suitable flat limit of the BPS Kerr-Newman-$AdS_4$ black hole in M-theory, we obtain a charged BPS solution in flat space. On the dual side, we determine a Carrollian-like limit on the known ABJM partition function on $S^1\times S^2$ at large $N$, and show that it reproduces the correct entropy of the BPS charged black hole in asymptotically flat space.

What Neural Network Field Theory Can and Cannot Realise on a Computer

arXiv:2608.21523 PDF hep-thcs.LGhep-lathep-phmath-ph
Thomas R. Harvey
한국어

neural network field theory의 한 가지 목표는 network ensemble 자체를 이론으로 삼아, quantum 또는 effective field theory를 컴퓨터 위에 구현하는 것이다. 우리는 계산 가능할 만큼 충분히 regular한 함수 클래스에 대해 이 목표가 얼마나 밀어붙여질 수 있는지 묻는다. 우리의 주요 결과는 표준 network architecture에 대해 성립하는 가정들을 갖는 no-go theorem이다. 우리는 이를 이용하여, 정의하는 대상이 finite width ensemble인지 그 infinite width limit인지, 그리고 계산하고자 하는 대상이 quantum field theory인지 effective field theory인지에 따라 neural network field theory의 네 가지 버전을 구분한다. 두 finite width 해석 모두 그 자체로 일관되지 않는다. 각 점에서 유한한 분산을 갖는 finite width ensemble의 경우, QFT 해석은 reflection positivity를 위반하며, EFT 해석은 그 positivity 위반을 유효성 영역 밖에 위치시킬 만한 scale separation을 확립하지 못한다. 두 가지 limit 버전 중 하나는 전체적으로 시뮬레이션될 수 있고, 다른 하나는 부분적으로만 시뮬레이션될 수 있는데, 이는 오직 그 smeared correlator만이 제어된 오차로 계산 가능하기 때문이다. 이와 같이, 제어된 수치 계산의 수준에서 QFT 버전과 EFT 버전은 구별될 수 없다. 한 차원에서는 이 장애를 벗어나지만, cosine network의 경우 그곳에서도 모든 유한한 width에서 reflection positivity가 여전히 위반됨이 보여진다. 이 theorem으로부터 벗어나는 두 가지 경로가 남아 있는데, 한 점에서의 유한한 분산을 포기하거나 정확한 rotation invariance를 포기하는 것이며, 우리는 이 두 가능성을 모두 논의한다.

Abstract

One aim of neural network field theory is to put a quantum or effective field theory on a computer, with the network ensemble itself as the theory. We ask how far that aim can be pushed for a function class regular enough to be computed with. Our main result is a no-go theorem with assumptions that hold for standard network architectures. We use it to separate four versions of neural network field theory, according to whether the defining object is the finite width ensemble or its infinite width limit, and whether the target we want to compute is a quantum or an effective field theory. Neither finite width interpretation is straightforwardly consistent. For finite width ensembles with finite variance at each point, the QFT interpretation fails reflection positivity, while the EFT interpretation establishes no scale separation by which the positivity violation can be placed outside its domain of validity. Of the two limit versions, one can be simulated in full and the other only in part, as only its smeared correlators are computable with a controlled error. As such, at the level of a controlled numerical computation, the QFT and EFT versions cannot be distinguished. One dimension escapes the obstruction, yet reflection positivity is shown to still fail there at every finite width for the cosine network. Two escapes from the theorem remain, giving up either finite variance at a point or exact rotation invariance, and we discuss both of these possibilities.

Neural Network Field Theory at Finite Width

arXiv:2608.21588 PDF hep-thcond-mat.dis-nncs.LG
Christian Ferko , Aaron Mutchler
한국어

완만한 가정 하에서, 임의의 quantum mechanical (QM) model이나 quantum field theory (QFT)는 가산 개의 random parameter를 갖는 neural network들의 ensemble로 표현될 수 있다. 우리는 유한 개의 parameter를 갖는 NN-QM 및 NN-FT model의 특징을 조사하는데, 예를 들어 width $N < \infty$인 feedforward network가 그것이다. 우리는 일반적으로 이러한 model들이 reflection positivity나 cluster decomposition과 같은 통상적인 Euclidean QFT의 성질 중 하나를 위반해야 함을 발견한다. 우리는 QM 및 QFT 양쪽에서 유한한 $N$에서 어떤 특징들이 보존될 수 있고 없는지를 이해하는 여러 상호 보완적인 방법을 제시한다.

Abstract

Under mild assumptions, any quantum mechanical (QM) model or quantum field theory (QFT) admits a representation in terms of an ensemble of neural networks with countably many random parameters. We investigate the features of NN-QM and NN-FT models with finitely many parameters, such as a feedforward network of width $N < \infty$. We find that, generically, such models must violate one of the properties of conventional Euclidean QFTs, such as reflection positivity or cluster decomposition. We present several complementary ways of understanding which features can and cannot be preserved at finite $N$, both in QM and in QFT.

Abstract

We study the radiative correction to the mass of the $(1+1)$-dimensional $\lambda\phi^4$ kink in the presence of an irreducible, spacelike Lorentz-violating interaction. The Lorentz-violating operator is chosen as $-\frac12 c\phi^2(u\!\cdot\!\partial\phi)^2$ with $u^\mu=(0,1)$, which is the lowest-order single-scalar operator considered in this class of models that produces effects that cannot be removed by a linear redefinition of coordinates or the field. We perform the one-loop renormalization of the underlying quantum field theory. In contrast with Lorentz-invariant $\lambda\phi^4$ theory in two dimensions, the Lorentz-violating theory requires, at this order, both a modified mass counterterm and a nonzero field-strength counterterm. The corrected stability problem contains modified bound and continuum modes, while the continuum phase shift remains unchanged at $O(c)$ with the boundary-condition prescription adopted here. Finally, using the vacuum-subtracted zero-point energy and the counterterm Hamiltonian, we obtain the one-loop kink mass through first order in $c$. We give the finite coefficient in the subtraction prescription implemented in the accompanying thesis calculation, for which the Lorentz-violating contribution is numerically negative.

Meissner scheme에서, pure gravity sector는 알려진 S-matrix 결과를 정확히 재현한다. 두 scheme 모두에서, pure gravity term은 double trace \((\Tr(R^2))^2\)로 표현될 수 있으며, 이에 대응하는 Yang-Mills term \((\Tr(F^2))^2\)와 결합하면, 문헌에서 예상되었던 대로 통합된 형태 \((\Tr(R^2-F^2))^2\)를 취한다.

Abstract

We utilize the standard T-duality procedure to derive the classical effective action of heterotic string theory at the eight-derivative order within the NS-NS sector, which comprises the metric, the \(B\)-field, and the dilaton. Starting from the minimal basis at this order, consisting of 872 even-parity and 477 odd-parity couplings, we perform a dimensional reduction on a circle and impose invariance under T-duality transformations, specifically the Buscher rules supplemented by higher-derivative corrections. This invariance uniquely fixes a subset of the couplings to match those of type II theory up to an overall factor, while the remaining couplings are determined in terms of the coefficients at order \(\alpha'\). For these latter couplings, we adopt both the Metsaev-Tseytlin and Meissner schemes. Subsequently, through field redefinitions, we recast the action into a canonical form in which the dilaton appears solely through the overall factor \(e^{-2\Phi}\). In the Meissner scheme, the pure gravity sector precisely reproduces the known S-matrix results. In both schemes, the pure gravity terms can be expressed as the double trace \((\Tr(R^2))^2\), and when combined with the corresponding Yang-Mills terms \((\Tr(F^2))^2\), they take the unified form \((\Tr(R^2-F^2))^2\), as anticipated in the literature.

Abstract

Thus the residue fixes one diagonal cone contribution but does not determine the term-complete characteristic response over the declared admissible coefficient domain.

Abstract

We study the inverse adjoint problem for a perturbative bosonic outgoing map. Within the polynomial bosonic canonical-commutation-relation (CCR) algebra, we prove a constructive integrability theorem: order-by-order preservation of the CCRs is sufficient to reconstruct recursively a formal self-adjoint generator whose exponential implements the outgoing map by adjoint conjugation. At each order, the Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff contribution determined by previously reconstructed generators is subtracted from the outgoing coefficient, and the remaining homogeneous CCR conditions determine the next generator explicitly. The theorem then reconstructs the self-adjoint generators (\delta T_1) and (\delta T_2), providing a formal perturbative unitary implementation. The resulting canonical organization also separates the direct (2\to2) contribution into irreducible-loop, one-particle-reducible, and factorized sectors.

Holographic SU(3) color superconductivity at finite baryon chemical potential

arXiv:2608.22013 PDF hep-thhep-ph

한국어

우리는 유한한 baryon chemical potential에서 diquark condensate에 의해 global color symmetry가 spontaneously broken되는 SU(3) color superconductivity (CSC)의 holographic model을 제시한다. $\mu_b=0$에서, 모든 SU(3) channel에 대한 체계적인 stability 분석은 알려진 SU(2) s+p 경쟁 관계를 복원한다. 이를 바탕으로, 유한한 $\mu_b$는 s-wave order를 S1+iS2 구조로 더 일반화한다. 우리는 $\mu_b$가 S1+iS2 phase의 critical temperature를 증가시키는 동시에 p-wave를 억제한다는 것을 발견하는데, backreaction이 포함되면 후자는 특정 $\mu_b$를 넘어서면서 사라진다. 이 반대되는 경향은 phase diagram을 재구성하는데, $\mu_b$가 증가함에 따라 균형 잡힌 S+ phase (S1=S2)가 color-superconducting region 전체를 차지하게 된다. 우리의 non-Abelian framework는 global SU(3) symmetry breaking에 대한 구체적인 holographic 기술을 제공하며, 이는 dense QCD에서의 color-superconducting phase를 이해하는 데 관련이 있고, neutron-star 내부의 물리학에 대한 새로운 통찰을 제공할 수 있다.

Abstract

We present a holographic model of SU(3) color superconductivity (CSC) where a global color symmetry is spontaneously broken by a diquark condensate at finite baryon chemical potential. Based on that, the finite $\mu_b$ further generalizes the s-wave order to an S1+iS2 structure. We find that $\mu_b$ enhances the critical temperature of the S1+iS2 phase while suppressing the p-wave -- the latter vanishes beyond a certain $\mu_b$ when backreaction is included. This opposite trend reshapes the phase diagram: with increasing $\mu_b$, the balanced S+ phase (S1=S2) takes over the entire color-superconducting region. Our non-Abelian framework provides a concrete holographic description of global SU(3) symmetry breaking, relevant for understanding color-superconducting phases in dense QCD and may offer new insights into the physics of neutron-star interiors.

Thermal propagator of the bosons and fermions fields
