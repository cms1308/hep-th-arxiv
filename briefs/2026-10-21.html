An Algebraic Formulation of Thermality in Nested Rindler Wedges

arXiv:2610.27007 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Waheed A. Dar , Prince A. Ganai , Nirmalya Kajuri
한국어

Padmanabhan과 Kolekar는 2차원 자유장론(free field theory)에서 Rindler vacuum이 Rindler observer에 대해 가속하는 관찰자에게 열적으로 나타나는 recursive thermality 현상을 보인 바 있다. 우리는 동일한 구성을 conformal field theory에 대해 대수적(algebraic) 수준에서 연구한다. 각 단계에서 parent wedge는 기준 Minkowski spacetime으로 conformal하게 사상(map)되며, 여기서 새로운 right Rindler wedge와 그 left sibling이 선택된다. 우리는 Bisognano--Wichmann property 하에서 모든 세대(generation)에서 thermality가 성립함을 보인다: parent wedge와 연관된 state는 그 right child로 제한될 때 KMS state가 된다. 더 나아가, wedge와 그 causal sibling의 modular structure를 이용하여 우리는 sibling algebra를 modular commutant와 동일시하고, 이에 대응하는 recursive modular flow를 명시적으로 유도한다.

Abstract

Padmanabhan and Kolekar had demonstrated the phenomenon of recursive thermality in two dimensional free field theories where the Rindler vacuum appears thermal to an observer accelerated with respect to the Rindler observer. We study the same construction at an algebraic level for conformal field theories. At each step, a parent wedge is conformally mapped to a reference Minkowski spacetime, where a new right Rindler wedge and its left sibling are selected. We illustrate that under the Bisognano--Wichmann property, thermality holds at every generation: the state associated with a parent wedge becomes a KMS state when restricted to its right child. Further, using the modular structure of the wedge and its causal sibling, we identify the sibling algebra with the modular commutant and derive the corresponding recursive modular flow explicitly.

Nambu-Goldstone Bosons and Quantum Fisher Information

arXiv:2610.27054 PDF hep-thcond-mat.otherhep-phquant-ph
Eugene Chen , Daniel Green
한국어

quantum Fisher matrix와 geometric tensor는 quantum system의 성질을 정량화하는 강력한 도구이다. 불행히도 이러한 양들을 계산하는 데 필요한 formalism은 불투명하여, ground state에서조차 이들의 거동에 대한 physical intuition의 발전을 제한해 왔다. continuous symmetry를 갖는 quantum system의 경우, ground state는 conserved current와 charge의 correlation function으로부터 model에 무관하고 직관적인 방식으로 이해될 수 있음이 알려져 있다. 본 논문에서 우리는 continuous symmetry를 갖는 system의 ground state와 연관된 quantum Fisher matrix와, 깨진 generator에 의해 생성되는 Nambu-Goldstone boson 사이의 동등성(equivalence)을 보인다. 우리는 Fisher matrix에 의해 정의되는 information metric이 그 system의 coset space를 정의하는 Zamolodchikov metric과 동일함을 보일 것이다. 다음으로 우리는 multi-parameter estimation과 연관된 Uhlmann curvature가 type-A와 type-B Nambu-Goldstone boson 사이의 차이를 정의하는 charge density matrix와 동일함을 보인다. 우리는 이러한 연관성을 여러 예제에서 보이고 finite temperature로의 일반화에 대해 논의한다.

Abstract

The quantum Fisher matrix and geometric tensor are powerful tools for quantifying the nature of quantum systems. Unfortunately, the formalism needed to compute these quantities is opaque and has limited the development of physical intuition for their behavior, even in the ground state. For quantum systems with continuous symmetries, we know the ground state can be understood in a model-independent and intuitive way from the correlation functions of conserved currents and charges. In this paper, we demonstrate an equivalence between the quantum Fisher matrix associated with the ground states of systems with continuous symmetries and the Nambu-Goldstone. bosons produced by broken generators. We will show that the information metric defined by the Fisher matrix is the same as the Zamolodchikov metric that defines the coset space of the system. Next we show that the Uhlmann curvature associated with multi-parameter estimation is the same as the charge density matrix that defines the difference between type-A and type-B Nambu-Goldstone bosons. We demonstrate these connections in several examples and discuss the generalization to finite temperature.

Scale-separated AdS vacua on Riemann-Flat Manifolds

arXiv:2610.27058 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Miquel Aparici , Ivano Basile , Bruno Valeixo Bento , Miguel Montero
한국어

flux와 Casimir energy에 의해 지지되는 supergravity compactification은 parametric scale separation과 soft supersymmetry breaking을 갖는 명시적이고 통제되며 isolated된 vacua를 구성하는 간단한 전략을 제공한다. moduli space에서 Laplacian operator에 대한 Casimir potential과 flux potential 양측의 특수한 성질을 활용하여, 우리는 임의의 flux가 관통하는 eleven-dimensional supergravity의 toroidal compactification으로 주어지는 가장 단순한 이러한 해의 종류가 항상 BF bound 아래의 tachyonic mode 형태의 perturbative instability를 나타냄을 증명한다. 이 no-go theorem은 $p$-form flux에 의해 지지되는 임의의 toroidal supergravity compactification에 적용된다. 우리는 또한 더 일반적인 Riemann-flat manifold(RFM)에서의 compactification을 연구한다. 구체적으로, 우리는 M-Theory를 $T^7/\mathbb{Z}_2^3$ 위에서 compactify함으로써 tachyon이 없는 AdS$_4$ vacuum을 찾는다. 이는 localized source 없이 완전히 top-down 방식이며 perturbatively stable하고 scale-separated된 AdS의 최초의 예시를 구성한다.

Abstract

Supergravity compactifications supported by fluxes and Casimir energy offer a simple strategy to concoct explicit, controlled and isolated vacua with parametric scale separation and soft supersymmetry breaking. Exploiting the special properties of both the Casimir and flux potentials with respect to Laplacian operators in moduli space, we prove that the simplest such class of solutions, given by toroidal compactifications of eleven-dimensional supergravity threaded by any fluxes, always exhibit perturbative instabilities in the form of tachyonic modes below the BF bound. This no-go theorem applies to any toroidal supergravity compactification supported by $p$-form fluxes. We also study compactifications on more general Riemann-flat manifolds (RFM's). Concretely, we find a tachyon-free AdS$_4$ vacuum by compactifying M-Theory on a $T^7/\mathbb{Z}_2^3$. This constitutes the first ever example of a fully top-down, perturbatively stable, scale-separated AdS without localized sources.

Bilocal flows and universality with field-dependent regulators

arXiv:2610.27063 PDF hep-thcond-mat.stat-mechgr-qc
Alfio M. Bonanno , Renata Ferrero
한국어

많은 functional renormalization group flow는 proper-time flow와 spectrally adjusted flow, 그리고 gauge theory와 quantum gravity의 background-independent formulation에서처럼 field에 의존하는 regulator를 사용한다. 이러한 regulator는 flow에 추가적인 항을 생성하며, 이를 생략하면 universality가 손상될 수 있다. 우리는 effective action이 field와 cutoff kernel을 독립변수로 하여 의존하는 bilocal flow를 도입한다. 이 구성은 추가적인 항들을 식별하며, exact sum rule은 anomalous dimension의 첫 번째 비자명한(nontrivial) 계수가 모든 허용 가능한 regulator profile에 대해 fixed-regulator flow의 계수와 일치함을 보여주어, universality가 회복된다. proper-time regulator를 갖는 massless $\lambda\phi^4$ theory의 경우, reduced flow는 profile에 의존하는 anomalous dimension을 주는 반면, completed flow는 모든 profile에 대해 universal한 two-loop 계수 $C_\eta=1/6$을 준다.

Abstract

Many functional renormalization group flows employ a regulator that depends on the fields, as in proper-time and spectrally adjusted flows and in background-independent formulations of gauge theories and quantum gravity. Such a regulator generates additional terms in the flow, and their omission may spoil universality. We introduce bilocal flows, in which the effective action depends on the field and on the cutoff kernel as independent variables. The construction identifies the additional terms, and an exact sum rule shows that the first nontrivial coefficient of the anomalous dimension coincides with that of a fixed-regulator flow for every admissible regulator profile, so that universality is restored. For massless $\lambda\phi^4$ theory with a proper-time regulator, the reduced flow gives a profile-dependent anomalous dimension, while the completed flow gives the universal two-loop coefficient $C_\eta=1/6$ for every profile.

한국어

Abstract

We investigate the nontrivial dynamics on $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls and compute their tension in quantum chromodynamics (QCD) compactified on a circle with $N$ colors and $F < N$ massless fundamental flavors. In the process, we obtain insights on the $\eta'$ meson's potential in a regime where it is heavy and comparable in mass to other QCD resonances, as in Nature. We employ a center-stabilized compactification of QCD on a small circle of size $L$ with color-flavor twisted boundary conditions for the quarks. We demonstrate that probe particles in color representations of $N$-ality $N/\mathrm{gcd}(N,F)$ are deconfined on the $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls whenever $N$ and $F$ are not coprime. This is reminiscent of deconfinement on axion domain walls in pure Yang--Mills. Finally, in the double scaling limit $N\rightarrow \infty$ keeping $NL$ and $F$ fixed, the infrared spectrum spans an emergent dimension. We establish that the $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls are dual to electrostatic charge distributions in this emergent dimension. This duality allows us to compute their tension analytically and find a different tension scaling at large-$N$ between $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls in QCD and axion domain walls in pure Yang--Mills, in center-stabilized compactifications.

Abstract

Gravitational path integral has had many applications in recent years. We also discuss to what extent we are led to string scale UV cut-off even if we begin with a supergravity theory without an a priori bias towards string theory. This is a review of mostly published results, but we clarify several details.

Abstract

We study classical soliton solutions in split-signature anti-self-dual Yang-Mills theory. Paul Balavoine , Severin Lüst
한국어

우리는 하나의 complex structure modulus를 갖는 Calabi-Yau fourfolds 위의 M-theory의 classical flux vacua를, 오직 asymptotic period 데이터와 monodromy 정보만을 이용하여 탐지하는 topological method를 개발한다. 이를 위해, 우리는 flux superpotential의 F-term의 index를 complex structure moduli space의 universal cover의 boundary를 따르는 winding number로 표현한다. 이 index는 적절히 정규화된 boundary F-term의 zero와 pole에 대한 합으로 계산되며, 여기서 local contribution은 boundary points의 조밀한 집합 위에서 대수적으로 계산될 수 있다. 나머지 contribution에 대한 추측된 bound를 가정하면, boundary F-term의 충분히 많은 sign change는 interior에 flux vacuum이 존재함을 보장한다. 우리는 이 방법을 mirror Fermat sextic에 대해 명시적인 예시와 체계적인 flux scan을 통해 설명한다. 우리의 구성에서 핵심적인 technical ingredient는 projective period map에 대한 continuity 결과이다: 우리는 monodromy representation이 relatively Anosov일 때 non-holomorphic (3,1)-period map이 covering space의 boundary로 연속적으로 확장됨을 증명한다. 일반적으로, 이 성질은 holomorphic (4,0)-period에 대해서는 성립하지 않는다.

Abstract

We develop a topological method to detect classical flux vacua of M-theory on Calabi-Yau fourfolds with a single complex structure modulus using only asymptotic period data and monodromy information. To this end, we express the index of the F-term of the flux superpotential as a winding number along the boundary of the universal cover of the complex structure moduli space. The index evaluates to a sum over zeros and poles of a suitably normalized boundary F-term, where local contributions can be computed algebraically on a dense set of boundary points. Assuming a conjectured bound on the remaining contributions, sufficiently many sign changes of the boundary F-term guarantee a flux vacuum in the interior. We illustrate the method on the mirror Fermat sextic with explicit examples and a systematic flux scan. A main technical ingredient in our construction is a continuity result for projective period maps: we prove that the non-holomorphic (3,1)-period map extends continuously to the boundary of the covering space if the monodromy representation is relatively Anosov. In general, this property does not hold for holomorphic (4,0)-periods.

Abstract

Whether string amplitudes can be characterized by extremality within the space of consistent S-matrices is an intriguing question.
