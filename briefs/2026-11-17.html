Schwarzschild scattering from CFT

arXiv:2611.18826 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Paolo Arnaudo , Benjamin Withers
한국어

우리는 AdS$_4$의 small black hole로 기술되는 상태에서 $\mathbb{R}\times \mathbb{S}^2$ 위의 retarded CFT correlator로부터 점근적으로 평탄한 Schwarzschild의 partial-wave S-matrix 원소, 그리고 quasinormal mode의 스펙트럼을 유도할 수 있음을 보인다. 이는 충분히 높은 진동수에서 쌍대인 bulk field가 AdS 경계 근처에서 평면파처럼 거동하는 연산자들의 부류를 규명함으로써 달성된다. 이러한 연산자들에 대해서는 CFT correlator와 반사 계수 사이에 간단한 해석적 관계가 존재하며, 이 관계는 flat space limit $L\to \infty$에서 매끄럽게 거동한다. 여기에는 spin-1, spin-2, 그리고 conformally coupled scalar가 포함된다. 또한 우리는 AdS의 small black hole과 매우 유사하게, Hermitian 계 내부에서도 짧은 시간 동안 dissipative QNM이 나타날 수 있는 방식을 포착하는 간단한 toy model을 제시한다.

Abstract

We show that retarded CFT correlators on $\mathbb{R}\times \mathbb{S}^2$ in a state described by the small black hole in AdS$_4$ can be used to derive asymptotically flat Schwarzschild partial-wave S-matrix elements, including its spectrum of quasinormal modes. This is achieved by identifying a class of operators for which the dual bulk fields behave as plane waves near the AdS boundary for sufficiently high frequencies. For such operators there is a simple analytic relation between CFT correlators and reflection coefficients which behaves gracefully in the flat space limit, $L\to \infty$. These include spin-1, spin-2, and conformally coupled scalars. We provide a simple toy model that captures how dissipative QNMs can appear for short times inside Hermitian systems, closely resembling the small black hole in AdS.

Rational conformal interfaces between the Ising and tricritical Ising models from a tetrality web

arXiv:2611.18831 PDF hep-thcond-mat.stat-mech
António Antunes , Jin Chen , Junchen Rong
한국어

우리는 서로 다른 chiral symmetry를 이용해 folded boundary state를 준비함으로써 Ising 모형과 tricritical Ising(TIM) 모형 사이의 rational conformal interface를 구성한다. 먼저 곱 이론을 $S_3$ automorphism group을 갖는 보조 coset으로 확장하여, 순열이 twisted Cardy state를 주는 moving up 절차를 사용한다. 여기에 더해 $S_3$ 불변 algebra로 환원하는 moving down 접근법을 보완적으로 사용한다. 종합하면 이러한 절차들은 네 가지 서로 다른 이론을 연결하는 tetrality web을 드러낸다. web에서 가장 작은 이론의 일반 Cardy state를 물리적인 tensor product 계로 pull back하면 열여덟 개의 factorized interface와 여섯 개의 transmitting interface를 얻는다. 후자는 가장 큰 이론에서의 네 개의 transposition twisted boundary 및 두 개의 3-cycle twisted boundary와 일치한다. 이들은 Gaiotto의 RG wall과, 여기에 Ising duality line을 fusion하여 얻은 interface를 포함한다. 이 여섯 interface는 TIM fusion ring의 regular action을 갖고 동일한 energy transmission coefficient $35/48$을 갖는다. 그러나 boundary entropy, bulk correlation, operator spectrum으로 이들을 구별할 수 있다. 우리는 이러한 observable들과 스물네 개의 boundary 사이의 완전한 annulus amplitude를 계산한다.

Abstract

We construct rational conformal interfaces between the Ising and tricritical Ising (TIM) models by using different chiral symmetries to prepare their folded boundary states. We use a moving up procedure which extends the product theory to an auxiliary coset with an $S_3$ automorphism group, where permutations give twisted Cardy states. We complement this with a moving down approach which reduces to the $S_3$ invariant algebra. Overall, these procedures uncover a tetrality web relating four different theories. Pulling back ordinary Cardy states of the smallest theory in the web to the physical tensor product system gives eighteen factorized interfaces and six transmitting interfaces. The latter agree with four transposition and two 3-cycle twisted boundaries in the largest theory. They include Gaiotto's RG wall and the interface obtained by fusing an Ising duality line into it. The six interfaces carry the regular action of the TIM fusion ring and have the same energy transmission coefficient, $35/48$. Their boundary entropies, bulk correlations and operator spectra distinguish them. We calculate these observables and the complete annulus amplitudes between the twenty four boundaries.

Constants and Nonperturbative Corrections in $V^{5,2}$ Theories

한국어

Abstract

We study perturbative and exponentially small corrections to the on-shell effective twisted superpotentials of two three-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ theories associated with free and fixed-locus cyclic quotients of the non-toric Sasaki-Einstein manifold $V^{5,2}$. These are the two-node Chern-Simons theory at levels $(k,-k)$ and the one-node flavored theory with $N_f$ fundamental pairs, respectively. At superconformal chemical potentials, we propose the Chern-Simons counterpart of the all-order perturbative expression for the flavored theory and obtain convergent integral representations for the rank-independent constants of both theories. At integer parameters, these constants admit explicit evaluations involving finite sums of Clausen functions and $\zeta(3)$, while their large-parameter expansions Borel sum to the defining integrals. At $k=1,3$ and $N_f=1,3$, we conjecture complete exponential remainders expressed through Eichler integrals of weight-four Eisenstein series. Supported by high-precision Bethe solutions, the resulting completions coincide at unit quotient order but differ at order three. We determine the minimal modular levels of the Eisenstein generators and establish convergence and natural boundaries for their primitives. Within the relative M-theory localization prescription, a complementary bulk analysis recovers the Chern-Simons shifted rank and constant in the $b\to0^+$ limit of the squashed-sphere partition function. We propose a generalization incorporating fixed-locus contributions that yields the corresponding flavored-theory result. The two theories therefore share a modular structure in their nonperturbative coefficient laws, but distinct perturbative shifts and exponential corrections resolve differences invisible in their common leading $N^{3/2}$ behavior.

Abstract

Kleiss--Kuijf and Bern--Carrasco--Johansson relations, and their string monodromy generalizations, have long provided some of the most powerful and useful constraints on partial amplitudes. Abstract

We study 6d $(1,0)$ SCFTs on a circle, possibly twisted, through the blow-up equations of their 5d descriptions. Abstract

Abstract

Bipartite entanglement entropy eventually saturates and therefore cannot track the continued growth of an eternal wormhole. Recently, it was shown that genuine tripartite multi-entropy can probe wormhole growth even after bipartite entanglement entropies have saturated. This naturally raises the question: can increasing the number of parties extend the probing time indefinitely? Adopting the multiway maximin prescription and assuming that its selected representatives are regular spacetime-stationary networks, we prove that the answer is no for nonrotating BTZ black holes. We establish a common finite upper bound on the probing time for every fixed finite boundary partition, independent of the number, sizes, and arrangement of its parties. For any network on any Cauchy slice, there exists a comparison network that assigns the entire central region containing the wormhole interior to a single party. A network longer than this comparison network cannot minimize length on that slice. The central length required by a stationary network connecting the two boundaries grows with time, whereas the central length of the comparison network remains uniformly bounded. At sufficiently late times, such a network therefore cannot minimize length on any Cauchy slice containing it and is excluded from the maximin prescription. Consequently, beyond this bound, no network selected by maximin enters the black-hole interior.

Abstract
