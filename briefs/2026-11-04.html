Linear Response in the $\mathcal{PT}$-Broken Phase: Conductivity of Fermions with Complex Conjugate Masses

arXiv:2611.03730 PDF hep-th
V. E. R. Lemes , D. G. Tedesco
한국어

우리는 fermion 질량들이 complex conjugate pair $m_{\pm}=N\pm iav$를 이루는 BRST invariant 모델의 $\PT$-broken phase에서의 linear response를 살펴본다. 이는 정확한(exact) $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$에 의해 분리되는 두 개의 channel로 나뉘며, 하나의 Euclidean correlator가 둘 다를 결정한다. diagonal channel에서는 그것이 Euclidean time에서 진동하므로 이 channel은 positive-norm realization을 허용하지 않는다: 우리는 negative norm을 갖는 test function을 제시하며, boundary value의 선택을 포함한 모든 수복(repair) 방법을 배제한다. mixed channel에서는 동일한 correlator가 completely monotone이며, 이는 spectral density가 positive임을 증명한다; 여기서 우리는 vacuum conductivity를 closed form으로 얻으며, $\omega=2N$에서 실수(real) threshold를 가지고, positivity는 그 개시점(onset)을 정확히 제시할 수 있는 어떤 bound 이하의 finite temperature에서도 유지된다. 그 channel의 longitudinal density는 mass splitting의 제곱만큼 negative이다; antisymmetric derivative가 이를 제거하며, 그 결과로 얻어지는 field-strength composite은 positive한 one-particle space와 causal commutator를 가진다 --- 다만 이는 two-point function 수준에서만 그러한데, 원래 composite에 대한 four-point reflection form은 negative로 나오기 때문이다. 따라서 이 positive realization은 fermion 모델의 sector가 아니다.

Abstract

We examine the linear response of the $\PT$-broken phase of a BRST invariant model whose fermion masses form the complex conjugate pair $m_{\pm}=N\pm iav$. It splits into two channels, kept apart by an exact $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$, and one Euclidean correlator decides both. In the diagonal channel it oscillates in Euclidean time, so that channel admits no positive-norm realization: we exhibit a test function of negative norm and close every repair, the choice of boundary value among them. In the mixed channel the same correlator is completely monotone, which proves the spectral density positive; there we obtain the vacuum conductivity in closed form, with a real threshold at $\omega=2N$, and positivity survives at finite temperature below a bound whose onset we give exactly. The longitudinal density of that channel is negative by the square of the mass splitting; an antisymmetric derivative removes it, and the resulting field-strength composite has a positive one-particle space and a causal commutator --- at the level of the two-point function only, since a four-point reflection form on the original composite comes out negative. The positive realization is therefore not a sector of the fermion model.

Meet biform singletons in your area! Topological actions for edge modes

arXiv:2611.03780 PDF hep-th
Calvin Y.-R. Chen , Euihun Joung , Karapet Mkrtchyan
한국어

우리는 biform singleton field를 edge mode로 propagate하는 topological field theory들을 제시한다. 짝수 spacetime dimension $D=2p$에서, 이들은 height $p$의 two-column Young diagram을 갖는 conformal algebra $\mathfrak{so}(2,2p)$의 irreducible representation이다. 이들의 duality 성질은 $p$의 parity에 따라 달라진다: $p$가 홀수(odd)일 때는 chiral degree of freedom을 propagate하고, $p$가 짝수(even)일 때는 electric--magnetic duality를 나타낸다. 우리는 singleton에 내재된 hidden conformal symmetry를 보조적인(auxiliary) bulk $\text{AdS}_{2p+1}$ geometry를 통해 기하학적으로 구현함으로써 topological action을 구성할 수 있다. 이를 출발점으로 삼아, 우리는 최근에 확립된 covariant boundary reduction procedure를 적용하여 manifest Lorentz covariance를 갖는 새로운 action formulation을 얻을 수 있다. 또한 우리는 manifest Lorentz covariance를 깨면 biform singleton의 알려진 formulation을 복원할 수 있음을 보인다.

Abstract

We present topological field theories propagating biform singleton fields as edge modes. In even spacetime dimensions $D=2p$, these are irreducible representations of the conformal algebra $\mathfrak{so}(2,2p)$ with two-column Young diagrams of height $p$. Their duality properties depend on the parity of $p$: They propagate chiral degrees of freedom for odd $p$, and exhibit electric--magnetic duality for even $p$. We are able to construct the topological actions by realising the hidden conformal symmetry inherent to singletons geometrically in terms of an auxiliary bulk $\text{AdS}_{2p+1}$ geometry. Using these as a starting point, we are able to apply a recently established covariant boundary reduction procedure to yield novel action formulations with manifest Lorentz covariance. We also show how, breaking manifest Lorentz covariance, we recover known formulations of biform singletons.

Worldsheet functional RG and the $α'$-tower

arXiv:2611.03781 PDF hep-th
Ivano Basile , Francesco Ferrarin , Alessia Platania , Matteo Zatti
한국어

우리는 bosonic string의 worldsheet theory에 functional renormalization group을 적용한다. local renormalization group flow에 대한 조건으로 Weyl anomaly cancellation을 부과하여, 우리는 일반적인 curved background 위에서 먼저 two-derivative truncation으로 background coupling에 대한 flow equation과 그에 대응하는 effective spacetime equation of motion을 유도한다. 이어서 우리는 $\alpha'$의 모든 order로의 truncation을 고려하는데, 이는 가능한 tensor structure 중 일부만을 포착하지만 $f(R)$-like approximation과 유사하게 analytically tractable하다. 이 truncation 내에서 우리는 Tseytlin의 spacetime effective action을 얻으며, 이를 이용하여 black hole을 포함한 $\alpha'$의 모든 order에서의 해(solution)를 연구한다. 우리의 결과는 functional renormalization group이 string worldsheet 위에서 구현될 수 있으며, strong-curvature regime에 접근할 수 있는 framework를 제공함을 보여준다. 더 장기적인 목표로서, 이는 string theory에서 effective asymptotic safety 시나리오를 검증할 수 있게 해줄 수 있는데, 이 시나리오에서는 low-energy effective field theory와 stringy completion 사이에 대략적으로 scale-invariant한 regime이 존재한다.

Abstract

We apply the functional renormalization group to the worldsheet theory of the bosonic string. Imposing the Weyl anomaly cancellation as a condition on the local renormalization group flow, we derive flow equations for the background couplings and the corresponding effective spacetime equations of motion, first in a two-derivative truncation, on a general curved background. We then consider a truncation to all orders in $\alpha'$, which captures only a subset of the possible tensor structures but is analytically tractable, in analogy with $f(R)$-like approximations. Within this truncation, we obtain Tseytlin's spacetime effective action, and we use it to study solutions to all orders in $\alpha'$, including black holes. Our results show that the functional renormalization group can be implemented on the string worldsheet and provide a framework to access the strong-curvature regime. As a longer-term goal, this may allow one to test the scenario of effective asymptotic safety in string theory, in which an approximately scale-invariant regime lies between the low-energy effective field theory and a stringy completion.

A New Holographic Duality for Conformal Mass Deformations

arXiv:2611.03784 PDF hep-th
Raphaël Dulac
한국어

우리는 half-space $z>0$ 위에서 ABJM의 공간에 의존하는(space-dependent) conformal mass deformation을 구성하고, 이 이론의 vacua가 superalgebra $D(2,1|\gamma)^{\oplus2}$를 가지며 $\gamma<0$로 parametrize되는, $AdS_3\times S^3\times S^3\times\Sigma$ 형태의 광범위한 half-BPS bubbling solution들과 dual함을 보인다. mass deformation이 $m(z)=\frac{c}{z}$로 scale될 경우 평면(plane)의 conformal symmetry가 보존된다. 우리는 ABJM의 inverse-distance mass deformation의 coefficient의 관점에서, M2-brane field theory에서 exceptional superalgebra parameter $\gamma$에 대한 최초의 field-theory 설명을 제공한다: $\gamma=\frac{1-2c}{1+2c}$. 우리는 이를 Polchinski와 Strassler의 방법을 사용하여 입증하는데, 이 방법을 먼저 $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang-Mills와 ABJM의 Janus interface에 적용하여 국소화된(localized) mass source를 scalar matching condition과 연관짓는다.

Abstract

We construct a space-dependent conformal mass deformation of ABJM on the half-space $z>0$ and show that the vacua of this theory are dual to a large class of half-BPS bubbling solutions of the form $AdS_3\times S^3\times S^3\times\Sigma$, with superalgebra $D(2,1|\gamma)^{\oplus2}$, parametrized by $\gamma<0$. The conformal symmetry of a plane is preserved provided that the mass deformation scales as $m(z)=\frac{c}{z}$. We give the first field-theory explanation of the exceptional superalgebra parameter $\gamma$ in M2-brane field theory in terms of the coefficient of the inverse-distance mass deformation of ABJM: $\gamma=\frac{1-2c}{1+2c}$. We demonstrate this using the methods of Polchinski and Strassler, which we first apply to the Janus interfaces of $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang-Mills and ABJM to relate localized mass sources to scalar matching conditions.

Chaos on the mass shell

arXiv:2611.03787 PDF hep-th
Alexandre Homrich , David Simmons-Duffin , George Yuzhe Zhou
한국어

우리는 UV에서 asymptotically free 또는 conformal한 theory와 IR에서 massive한 theory 사이의 RG flow로 주어지는 이론들에 대해, high-energy state에서의 particle production을 연구한다. detector matching의 framework를 사용하여, 우리는 생성된 particle들의 distribution이 UV theory의 light-ray operator의 spectrum에 의해 결정되는, energy에 대한 universal evolution equation을 만족함을 논증한다. 특히, 우리는 UV Chew-Frautschi plot을 mass shell 위에서 energy에 따라 진화하는 particle density에 대한 wave equation으로 해석한다. (UV theory가 CFT일 때, 이 wave equation은 특정 temperature에서 hyperbolic space에서의 chaos의 확산을 제어하는 것과 동일한 방정식이다.) 우리는 2d CFT에서의 integrable flow에 대해 이러한 아이디어를 검증하며, Feynman gas의 thermodynamics의 관점에서 evolution equation에 대한 독립적인 유도를 발견한다. 우리는 또한 outgoing wave of hadron number density에 의해 생성되는 BFKL contrail의 존재를 포함하여, 4d gauge theory에 대한 함의(consequence)도 논의한다.

Abstract

We study particle production in high-energy states for theories given by an RG flow between an asymptotically free or conformal theory in the UV and a massive theory in the IR. Using the framework of detector matching, we argue that distributions of produced particles satisfy universal evolution equations with energy, determined by the spectrum of light-ray operators of the UV theory. In particular, we interpret the UV Chew-Frautschi plot as a wave equation for particle densities on the mass shell, evolving with energy. (When the UV theory is a CFT, this wave equation is the same one that controls the spread of chaos in hyperbolic space at a particular temperature.) We test these ideas for integrable flows in 2d CFTs, finding an independent derivation of the evolution equations in terms of the thermodynamics of a Feynman gas. We also discuss consequences for 4d gauge theories, including the presence of a BFKL contrail produced by an outgoing wave of hadron number density.

Inner Product of Closed Universe from Gravitational Path-integral

arXiv:2611.03789 PDF hep-th
Masamichi Miyaji , Sunil Sake , Kotaro Shinmyo
한국어

우리는 gravitational path integral로부터, anti-de Sitter (AdS) 및 de Sitter (dS) Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT) gravity에서 고정된 topology를 갖는 closed universe의 Hilbert space를 구성한다. gravity를 quantize하는 것은 lapse contour의 선택, path-integral measure, operator ordering, gauge group의 global structure, gravitational constraint의 구현, 그리고 초기 및 최종 timeslice에서의 gauge symmetry를 포함한 여러 미묘한(subtle) 문제들을 수반한다. 우리는 이러한 문제들을 다루고 physical state의 inner product를 결정하는 formalism을 개발하여, cosmological wavefunction에 확률을 부여할 수 있는 framework를 제공한다. 고정된 topology와 lapse contour에 대해, 우리는 inner product를 정확히 계산하고 JT gravity에서 그에 대응하는 resolution of the identity를 얻는다. 우리는 refined algebraic quantization에서의 co-invariant 및 invariant Hilbert space가 초기 및 최종 timeslice에서의 특정한 gauge fixing으로부터 발생함을 보인다. 특히, 이 formalism은 고정된 geometric data를 갖는 gravitational wavefunction에 대해, 그 integral measure를 포함하여 co-invariant state로서의 정확한 의미를 제공한다. 이러한 구성들은 Hamiltonian BFV-BRST formalism 내에서 formulate될 수 있다. 우리는 또한 BF theory의 그것과는 다르고 big bang/crunch 근처에서 채택된 prescription에 의존하는, Hilbert space의 global structure에 대해서도 논의한다. 우리의 결과는 cylinder geometry가 Hartle-Hawking no-boundary state를 포함한 모든 perturbative state에 대한 gravitational inner product를 포착함을 시사한다.

Abstract

We construct the Hilbert space of a closed universe of fixed topology in anti-de Sitter (AdS) and de Sitter (dS) Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT) gravity from the gravitational path integral. Quantizing gravity involves several subtleties, including the choice of lapse contour, path-integral measure, operator ordering, global structure of the gauge group, the implementation of gravitational constraints and the gauge symmetries at initial and final timeslices. We develop a formalism that addresses these issues and determines the inner product of physical states, providing a framework to assign probabilities to cosmological wavefunctions. For a fixed topology and a lapse contour, we compute the inner product exactly and obtain the corresponding resolution of the identity in JT gravity. We demonstrate that the co-invariant and invariant Hilbert spaces in refined algebraic quantization arise from particular gauge fixing at the initial and final timeslices. In particular, the formalism provides precise meaning for a gravitational wavefunction with fixed geometric data as a co-invariant state, including its integral measure. These constructions can be formulated within the Hamiltonian BFV-BRST formalism. We also discuss the global structure of the Hilbert space, which differs from that of BF theory and depends on the prescription adopted near the big bang/crunch. Our results imply that the cylinder geometry captures the gravitational inner product for all perturbative states, including the Hartle-Hawking no-boundary state.

Compact holography via polarized IKKT

arXiv:2611.03807 PDF hep-th
Arsam Najafian , Mark Van Raamsdonk , Alejandro Vilar López
한국어

polarized IKKT matrix model은 Euclidean type IIB string theory의 $N$ D-instanton sector와 dual한 것으로 제안된 $U(N)$ supersymmetric matrix integral이다. 우리는 matrix model saddles로부터 supergravity saddles로 가는 직접적인 경로를 보인다: matrix model resolvent $\to$ holomorphic function $\to$ 2D Coulomb potential $\to$ 4D Coulomb potential $\to$ supergravity fields, 여기서 마지막 단계는 이전에 기술된 바 있다. 여기서 2D electrostatics picture는 배경장 속에서 dipoles의 constrained gas를 주는 matrix model의 localization deformation을 정확히 finite $N$으로 재구성한 것이다. 우리는 모든 saddles에 대해 supergravity와 matrix model의 on-shell actions 및 protected observables 사이의 일치를 보인다. 우리는 supergravity saddles가 string frame에서 compact하고 non-singular한 metric을 가지며, Einstein frame의 asymptotic region이 pointlike D-instanton source로 대체됨을 보인다. 우리는 특정 gravity saddles의 물리가 대응하는 matrix model saddle과 연관된 basin으로 matrix model integral의 domain을 제한함으로써 얻어질 수 있다는 증거를 제시한다. 우리는 saddles의 family가 나머지로부터 decouple되어 특정 고차원 field theories의 gravity duals를 주는 특정 large $N$ decoupling limits를 기술한다.

Abstract

The polarized IKKT matrix model is a $U(N)$ supersymmetric matrix integral proposed to be dual to an $N$ D-instanton sector of Euclidean type IIB string theory. We show a direct path from matrix model saddles to supergravity saddles: matrix model resolvent $\to$ holomorphic function $\to$ 2D Coulomb potential $\to$ 4D Coulomb potential $\to$ supergravity fields, where the last step was previously described. Here, the 2D electrostatics picture is an exact finite $N$ reformulation of a localization deformation of the matrix model that gives a constrained gas of dipoles in a background field. We show a matching between supergravity and matrix model on-shell actions and protected observables for all saddles. We show that the supergravity saddles have a compact and non-singular metric in string frame with the Einstein frame asymptotic region replaced by a pointlike D-instanton source. We provide evidence that the physics of particular gravity saddles can be obtained through restricting the domain of the matrix model integral to a basin associated with the corresponding matrix model saddle. We describe certain large $N$ decoupling limits where families of the saddles decouple from the rest to give the gravity duals of certain higher-dimensional field theories.

Resonant electroweak clouds around Schwarzschild black holes

arXiv:2611.03809 PDF hep-th

한국어

우리는 bosonic Weinberg-Salam theory에서 Schwarzschild black holes 주위의 nonlinear electroweak clouds를 구성한다. 이 구성들은 harmonically time-dependent한 charged gauge fields에 의해 지지되며, 그들의 energy-momentum tensor는 static으로 유지된다. horizon에서의 regularity는 field frequency와 horizon electrostatic potential을 연관시키는 resonance condition을 부과한다. linear clouds와 달리, 이 solutions는 유한한 mass와 electric charge gap을 보이며, 오직 최대 horizon size까지만 존재한다. 우리는 spherically symmetric 및 dipolar 구성을 포함한 magnetic clouds와 electric clouds를 모두 얻으며, 이들은 vanishing-horizon limit에서 electroweak balls로 환원된다. 우리는 또한 dipolar electroweak balls의 새로운 family를 보고하는데, 이는 고정된 electric charge에서 spherical한 대응 구성보다 에너지적으로 선호될 수 있다. 측정된 값에서 벗어난 electroweak couplings에 대해 probe limit에서 얻어진 우리의 결과는 non-Abelian gauge-Higgs theory에서 nonlinear resonant vector hair의 새로운 실현을 확립한다.

Abstract

We construct nonlinear electroweak clouds around Schwarzschild black holes in the bosonic Weinberg-Salam theory. These configurations are supported by harmonically time-dependent charged gauge fields, while their energy-momentum tensor remains static. Regularity at the horizon imposes a resonance condition relating the field frequency to the horizon electrostatic potential. Unlike linear clouds, these solutions exhibit a finite mass and electric charge gap, and exist only up to a maximal horizon size. We obtain both magnetic and electric clouds, including spherically symmetric and dipolar configurations, which reduce to electroweak balls in the vanishing-horizon limit. We also report a new family of dipolar electroweak balls, which can be energetically favoured over their spherical counterparts at fixed electric charge. Our results, obtained in the probe limit for electroweak couplings away from their measured values, establish a new realization of nonlinear resonant vector hair in a non-Abelian gauge-Higgs theory.

Abstract

One purpose of reconstructing the conformal algebra is to identify conformal primary states, which we do with some success. We also find further evidence supporting the model of the short-distance corrections to the entanglement Hamiltonian proposed earlier and use it to make systematic improvements.

A lattice construction of the Kormos-Runkel-Watts defect flows in minimal models

arXiv:2611.03824 PDF hep-th

Kormos, Runkel, Watts (KRW)는 diagonal minimal model $M(p,p+1)$의 topological defect가 weakly relevant한 defect field에 의해 교란될 때 새로운 conformal interface로 흐른다는 것을 보였다. 우리는 spinful defect field $\phi_{(1,3)}\otimes\bar\phi_{(3,3)}$에 의해 유도되는 $(2,1)$ Verlinde defect $D_{(2,1)}$의 flow를 lattice 위에서 연구한다. 이 flow는 $(r,1)$ Verlinde lines를 보존한다. 이 보존된 symmetry는 dilute RSOS realisation을 가능하게 한다. 우리는 exact diagonalization과 density matrix renormalization group (DMRG)을 모두 사용하여 이 flow를 수치적으로 연구한다. $p=4$에서, flow는 invertible line $\eta=D_{(3,1)}$에서 끝난다. $p=5$에서는 $g=1.503(2)$이고 transmission coefficient $T=0.665(10)$인 non-trivial conformal interface $C_5$에서 끝난다. 우리는 그 spectrum, leading irrelevant dimension, 그리고 lattice $\beta$-function을 보고한다. $\eta$를 gauging하면 $C_5$는 three-state Potts model의 interface로 사상되는데, 이는 $\mathbb{Z}_3$을 깨뜨리지만 Fibonacci line $\tau_{\mathrm{Fib}}$와 charge conjugation이 부과되면 stable한 interface가 된다. loop-model formulation을 통해 우리는 연속적인 $4\le p\le5$에서 family $C_p$를 추적할 수 있다: $p$를 낮추면 $C_5$에서 $\eta$로 매끄럽게 이동한다.

Kormos, Runkel, and Watts (KRW) showed that a topological defect of a diagonal minimal model $M(p,p+1)$, perturbed by a weakly relevant defect field, flows to a new conformal interface. This map rewrites a continuum Hamiltonian as a lattice Hamiltonian with nearest-neighbor couplings and an infinite-dimensional local Hilbert space. The resulting lattice model reproduces exactly the energy spectrum, global operator algebra, chiral symmetries, and anomalies of the continuum QFT on any finite lattice. Unlike domain wall fermions, truncating the local Hilbert space introduces no extra massless modes, and the entanglement entropy of an interval converges as the truncation is removed. The lattice local algebras are of type I and lie between the type III continuum algebras of nested spatial regions, in accordance with the split property. We demonstrate this construction explicitly for $N$ Majorana fermions, realizing the $\mathrm{O}(N)_\mathrm{L}\times\mathrm{O}(N)_\mathrm{R}$ lattice chiral symmetry. We also construct an exact lattice model for the Schwinger model and numerically reproduce the mass gap $e/\sqrt{\pi}$.

Abstract

We construct boundary Liouville conformal field theory at central charge $c\leq1$ with FZZT-type boundary conditions and show that it satisfies the Cardy--Lewellen sewing constraints on the disk and the annulus. Using the analytic bootstrap we derive the so far missing boundary three-point and bulk-to-boundary structure constants and a consistent disk one-point function as explicit meromorphic functions of the Liouville momenta. In sharp contrast to $c\geq25$, the open-string spectrum between two ``timelike'' FZZT boundaries is \emph{discrete}, yet non-trivially consistent through open-closed duality with the \emph{continuous} closed-string spectrum of Ribault and Santachiara.
