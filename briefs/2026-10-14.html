Semiclassical state on algebra of observables for de Sitter space and time reversal symmetry

arXiv:2610.14175 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Muxin Han , Yichen Hu
한국어

de Sitter static patch holography에서 bulk quantum field를 observer의 동역학적 clock에 결합하면 gauge-invariant observable들로 이루어진 type II$_1$ von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{M}_+$가 얻어진다. 그러나 이 algebra 위의 유일한 tracial state는 semiclassical bulk correlation function을 재현하지 못한다. 우리는 무거운 관성을 가진 observer reference frame을 나타내는 heavy-clock state들의 족을 도입하여 이 문제를 해결한다. heavy-clock limit에서 gauge-invariant dressed observable들의 $n$-point correlation function이 de Sitter 시공간 위의 bulk quantum field correlation function을 재현함을 증명한다. 나아가 순방향($\pi\geq 0$) clock과 역방향($\pi <0$) clock을 모두 포함하는 일반화된 observer Hamiltonian $\widehat{H}_{\rm obs}=|\widehat{\pi}|$를 조사한다. 그 결과로 얻어지는 gauge-invariant observable들의 von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{M}$은 여전히 type~II$_1$ factor이며 $\mathcal{M}_+\otimes M_2(\mathbb{C})$와 동형이다. 여기서 $M_2(\mathbb{C})$는 두 clock 방향 사이를 변환하는 $2\times2$ 복소 행렬들의 algebra이다. 이 설정에서 time-reversal symmetry는 두 clock 방향을 맞바꾸는 anti-unitary operator로 구현되며, gauge-invariant하고 time-reversal-invariant한 observable들의 type~II$_1$ von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{F}$를 낳는다. 우리는 heavy-clock state 구성을 $\mathcal{M}$과 $\mathcal{F}$ 모두로 일반화하여, heavy-clock state에서 계산한 $n$-point correlation function이 대응하는 semiclassical bulk correlation function과 일치함을 보인다. 마지막으로 Einstein gravity를 Brown--Kuchař dust field에 결합하여, $G \to 0$ limit에서 observer의 worldline을 따른 perturbative reduction으로부터 observer Hamiltonian $\widehat{H}_{\rm obs}=|\widehat{\pi}|$를 유도한다. 이는 본 연구에서 전개한 observer model에 대한 field-theoretic 기반을 제공한다.

Abstract

In de Sitter static patch holography, coupling bulk quantum fields to an observer's dynamical clock yields a type II$_1$ von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{M}_+$ of gauge-invariant observables. However, the unique tracial state on this algebra fails to reproduce semiclassical bulk correlation functions. We resolve this issue by introducing a family of heavy-clock states, representing an observer reference frame with heavy inertia. We prove that in the heavy-clock limit, $n$-point correlation functions of gauge-invariant dressed observables reproduce the bulk quantum field correlation functions on de Sitter spacetime. Furthermore, we investigate a generalized observer Hamiltonian $\widehat{H}_{\rm obs}=|\widehat{\pi}|$ that incorporates both forward-running ($\pi\geq 0$) and backward-running ($\pi <0$) clocks. The resulting von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{M}$ of gauge-invariant observables remains a type~II$_1$ factor and is isomorphic to $\mathcal{M}_+\otimes M_2(\mathbb{C})$, where $M_2(\mathbb{C})$ is the algebra of $2\times2$ complex matrices transforming between the two clock orientations. Time-reversal symmetry is implemented in this setting as an anti-unitary operator that exchanges the two clock orientations, yielding a type~II$_1$ von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{F}$ of gauge-invariant, time-reversal-invariant observables. We generalize the heavy-clock state construction to both $\mathcal{M}$ and $\mathcal{F}$, showing that $n$-point correlation functions calculated in heavy-clock states match the corresponding semiclassical bulk correlation functions. Finally, by coupling Einstein gravity to the Brown--Kuchař dust field, we derive the observer Hamiltonian $\widehat{H}_{\rm obs}=|\widehat{\pi}|$ from a perturbative reduction along the observer's worldline in the $G \to 0$ limit. This provides a field-theoretic foundation for the observer model developed in this work.

Defect conformal field theories with free-in-the-bulk fermions

arXiv:2610.14176 PDF hep-th
Christopher P. Herzog , Daniel Lloyd-Jones
한국어

우리는 4차원 bulk에서는 free이지만 2차원 defect 위에서는 상호작용하는 massless fermion에 대한 codimension-two conformal defect를 연구한다. bulk Dirac equation은 defect conformal field theory 데이터를 강하게 제약한다. 이는 bulk-defect operator product expansion에 나타나는 operator의 dimension을 고정하며, bulk-defect-defect three-point function에 대해서는 shadow pair 중 한 쪽만 unitarity에 의해 허용될 때 double-twist 관계를 강제한다. monodromy defect의 lowest mode는 중요한 예외인데, 두 shadow partner가 모두 unitary일 수 있으며 이들의 three-point function은 대신 shadow transform으로 연결되기 때문이다. 우리는 정확히 풀리는 bilinear flow와 Gross-Neveu형 quartic coupling을 포함한 defect interaction으로 이러한 구조를 예시한다. perturbative mixed fixed point에서, double-twist 조건이 위배되고 두 shadow channel이 결합하여 regular한 bulk-defect-defect correlation function을 주는 명시적인 interacting 예를 찾는다.

Abstract

We study codimension-two conformal defects for massless fermions that are free in a four-dimensional bulk, but interact on a two-dimensional defect. The bulk Dirac equation strongly constrains defect conformal field theory data: it fixes the dimensions of operators in the bulk-defect operator product expansion and, for bulk-defect-defect three-point functions, enforces a double-twist relation when only one member of a shadow pair is allowed by unitarity. The lowest modes of monodromy defects provide an important exception, since both shadow partners can be unitary and their three-point functions are instead related by a shadow transform. We illustrate these structures with defect interactions, including an exactly tractable bilinear flow and Gross-Neveu-type quartic couplings. At a perturbative mixed fixed point, we find an explicit interacting example in which the double-twist condition is violated and the two shadow channels combine to give a regular bulk-defect-defect correlation function.

The dimensional reduction of the spin-3 and spin-4 partially broken Fronsdal models

arXiv:2610.14284 PDF hep-th
D. Dalmazi , A. L. R. dos Santos
한국어

여기서는 spin-3 및 spin-4에 대한 partially broken Fronsdal model을 $D+1$차원에서 $D$차원으로 Kaluza-Klein dimensional reduction한다. reduction된 model은 추가적인 propagating mode 없이 massive spin-3 및 spin-4 입자를 기술한다. 이들은 higher dimensional massless parent와 마찬가지로 on shell에서 사라지는 quadratic term에 의해 Singh-Hagen model과 달라진다. reduction된 model을 gauge invariant한 field combination으로 covariant하게 기술하면, 낮은 spin의 경우($s\le 2$)와 달리 자연스럽게 higher derivative model로 이어진다. $s=3$과 $s=4$ 모두에서 상당히 특이한 massless limit이 나타난다. 입자 content는 예상대로 $j=0,1, \cdots s$인 $\pm j$ helicity에 해당하지만, 이들은 비자명한 방식으로 얽혀 있어 어떠한 local field redefinition으로도 대각화할 수 없다.

Abstract

Here we perform a Kaluza-Klein dimensional reduction from $D+1$ to $D$ dimensions of a partially broken Fronsdal model for spin-3 and spin-4. The reduced models describe massive spin-3 and spin-4 particles without extra propagating modes. They differ from Singh-Hagen models by quadratic terms which vanish on shell, similarly to their higher dimensional massless parents. A covariant description of the reduced models in terms of gauge invariant field combinations naturally leads to higher derivative models, contrary to lower spin cases ($s\le 2$). For both $s=3$ and $s=4$ we have a rather peculiar massless limit. Although their particle content corresponds to the $\pm j$ helicities with $j=0,1, \cdots s$, as expected, they appear intertwined in a non trivial way preventing diagonalization through any local field redefinition.

Three-dimensional vacua of $G_2$ flux compactifications

arXiv:2610.14313 PDF hep-th
Aravind Aikot , Julian Choi , Timm Wrase
한국어

우리는 $G_2$-structure 공간 위의 type IIA, type IIB/type I, heterotic compactification으로부터 얻어지는 3차원 $\mathcal N=1$ effective theory의 supersymmetric vacua를 연구한다. superpotential의 homogeneity와 physical scalar metric으로부터, 전체 moduli-space metric을 specify하지 않고도 보편적인 vacuum 관계와 axion stabilization 제약을 얻는다. AdS$_3$ scalar mass를 dual 2차원 conformal field theory에서 대응하는 operator의 scaling dimension인 $\Delta$로 표현한다. massive type IIA AdS$_3$ vacua에서 bound $\Delta_{\max}\geq(20+4\sqrt{67})/7\approx 7.5$를 유도하고, torsion-free branch에서 $B_2$ axion의 정확한 dimension $\Delta_b=6$을 얻는다. Type IIB type I 및 heterotic AdS$_3$ vacua는 $\Delta_{\phi s}=4$인 보편적 volume-dilaton mode를 포함한다. singlet-torsion type IIB branch에서는 $\Delta_{\phi s}=20/7$인 volume-dilaton mode와 $\Delta_b=16/7$도 얻는다. 고려한 classical closed-string theory에서 모든 supersymmetric Minkowski vacuum는 정확한 volume-dilaton flat direction을 가진다. 우리는 tadpole 제약과 axionic identification 이후의 물리적인 large-flux limit을 규명하고, isotropic scale separation에 대한 torsion obstruction을 찾아낸다.

Abstract

We study supersymmetric vacua of three-dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ effective theories from type IIA, type IIB/type I, and heterotic compactifications on $G_2$-structure spaces. Superpotential homogeneity and the physical scalar metric yield universal vacuum relations and axion stabilization constraints without specifying the full moduli-space metric. We express AdS$_3$ scalar masses through $\Delta$, the scaling dimension of the corresponding operator in the dual two-dimensional conformal field theory. In massive type IIA AdS$_3$ vacua, we derive the bound $\Delta_{\max}\geq(20+4\sqrt{67})/7\approx 7.5$ and an exact dimension $\Delta_b=6$ for $B_2$ axions on the torsion-free branch. Type IIB type I and heterotic AdS$_3$ vacua contain a universal volume-dilaton mode with $\Delta_{\phi s}=4$. On the singlet-torsion type IIB branch, we also obtain the volume-dilaton mode with $\Delta_{\phi s}=20/7$ and $\Delta_b=16/7$. Every supersymmetric Minkowski vacuum in the retained classical closed-string theories possesses an exact volume-dilaton flat direction. We characterize physical large-flux limits after tadpole constraints and axionic identifications, and identify torsion obstructions to isotropic scale separation.

One-loop QED effective action at finite temperature and density beyond HTL limit

arXiv:2610.14345 PDF hep-th
Farahmand Hasanov , Nikita Kolganov
한국어

우리는 임의의 짝수 시공간 차원에서 유한 온도와 chemical potential을 가진 scalar 및 spinor QED의 nonlocal one-loop effective action을 계산하는 framework를 개발한다. background field에 대해 quadratic order까지의 heat kernel expansion에 기반하여, 이 framework는 effective action의 form factor들을 하나의 공통된 fundamental kernel로 환원한다. external-momentum expansion을 resummation함으로써, polylogarithm과 order에 대한 그 미분으로 이루어진 massless form factor의 명시적인 closed-form 표현을 얻는다. 같은 방법으로 small-mass expansion의 계수도 임의의 order까지 결정된다. 얻어진 표현은 soft-momentum approximation을 사용하지 않고 external Euclidean momentum, 온도, chemical potential에 대한 전체 의존성을 유지한다. 또한 대응하는 photon polarization operator를 유도하고 그 static limit을 계산하여 Debye screening mass와 electromagnetic susceptibility를 얻는다.

Abstract

We develop a framework for computing the nonlocal one-loop effective action in scalar and spinor QED at finite temperature and chemical potential in arbitrary even spacetime dimensions. Based on the heat kernel expansion to quadratic order in background fields, the framework reduces the effective action form factors to a common fundamental kernel. By resumming the external-momentum expansion, we obtain explicit closed-form expressions for the massless form factors in terms of polylogarithms and their derivatives with respect to the order. The same method determines the coefficients of the small-mass expansion to arbitrary order. The resulting expressions retain the full dependence on external Euclidean momentum, temperature, and chemical potential, without invoking the soft-momentum approximation. We also derive the corresponding photon polarization operators and evaluate their static limits, obtaining the Debye screening masses and electromagnetic susceptibilities.

Characteristic Modes of Non-minimally Coupled Thick Branes

arXiv:2610.14430 PDF hep-thgr-qchep-ex
Jing-Xin Gong , Heng Guo , Yong-Tao Lu , Xin-Yi Pan
한국어

Characteristic mode는 중력계의 고유한 성질과 동역학적 응답에 관한 중요한 정보를 제공한다. 본 연구에서는 non-minimally coupled bulk scalar field를 가진 thick brane의 characteristic mode를 조사한다. 각각 single-kink scalar configuration과 내부 구조를 가진 deformed kink configuration에 해당하는 두 가지 대표적인 thick brane 해를 고려한다. tensor perturbation equation을 분석하여, 먼저 두 brane model에서 graviton zero mode의 localization을 살펴본다. 두 brane model 모두에서 graviton zero mode는 brane에 localize된다. $\xi=1/7$인 single-kink brane에서는 뚜렷한 resonant Kaluza--Klein (KK) mode가 발견되지 않는다. deformed brane에 대해서는 $\xi=0.99$에서 resonant KK mode를 조사하여, deformation parameter $k$가 증가하면 resonant mode가 더 많아지고 첫 번째 resonance의 수명이 더 길어짐을 발견한다. 이어서 두 brane model의 quasinormal mode (QNM)를 frequency domain과 time domain에서 독립적으로 계산한다. 독립적인 방법으로 얻은 quasinormal frequency (QNF)는 낮은 overtone에서 잘 일치한다. resonant KK mode의 질량과 decay rate는 대응하는 QNF의 실수부와 허수부의 크기에 각각 매우 가깝게 일치한다.

Abstract

Characteristic modes provide important information about the intrinsic properties and dynamical response of gravitational systems. In this work, we investigate characteristic modes of thick branes with a non-minimally coupled bulk scalar field. Two representative thick brane solutions are considered, corresponding to a single-kink scalar configuration and a deformed kink configuration with internal structure, respectively. By analyzing the tensor perturbation equations, we first examine the localization of the graviton zero mode in the two brane models. For both brane models, the graviton zero mode is localized on the brane. No pronounced resonant Kaluza--Klein (KK) modes are found for the single-kink brane at $\xi=1/7$. For the deformed brane, we investigate the resonant KK modes at $\xi=0.99$, finding that increasing the deformation parameter $k$ leads to more resonant modes and a longer-lived first resonance. We then calculate the quasinormal modes (QNMs) of both brane models independently in the frequency and time domains. The quasinormal frequencies (QNFs) obtained by the independent methods agree well for the low overtones. The masses and decay rates of the resonant KK modes agree closely with the real parts and the magnitudes of the imaginary parts of the corresponding QNFs, respectively.

Haagerup conformal field theory at central charge eight

한국어

Abstract

It is generated by two commuting affine $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ current algebras at levels $1$ and $39$, the stress tensor of the lattice theory, and a single weight-two field built from three lattice exponentials. We announce that $U$ is strongly rational and completely unitary, and that $Rep(U)$ is equivalent, as a unitary ribbon category, to the Drinfeld center $Z(\mathcal{H}_3)$ of the Haagerup fusion category $\mathcal{H}_3$. Its characters are the $\gamma=0$ Evans--Gannon vector. From $U$ we obtain a diagonal full CFT, and also a holomorphic $E_8$ extension on which $\mathcal{H}_3$ acts by topological defects.

holographic theory를 그 equation of state로부터 재구성할 수 있을까? 우리는 Pilch--Warner model의 bosonic-mass sector에서 physics-informed neural network를 이용하여 이 질문을 조사한다. 먼저 conformal equation of state에 대한 leading high-temperature correction을 담은 해석해와 비교하여 PINN 접근법을 검증한다. 이어서 thermodynamics, ultraviolet asymptotics, Ward identity, horizon regularity를 이용하여 nonlinear black-brane geometry와 scalar potential을 재구성한다. bulk Einstein-scalar equation의 독립적인 해는, 훈련된 PINN으로 thermodynamics를 정확히 재현하더라도 scalar potential의 정확한 재구성이 보장되지는 않음을 보여준다. high-temperature equation of state에 대한 고정밀 지식은 bulk scalar potential의 nonlinear interaction을 제약한다. 이러한 high-temperature 추출에 착안하여 cubic coupling을 정확한 PW 값으로 고정하면, bulk scalar mass만 제약한 경우에 비해 재구성된 scalar self-interaction의 최대 sampled error가 약 15배 줄어든다. gauge theory plasma의 bulk viscosity에 대한 지식을 추가하면 이 오차는 다시 약 13배 더 줄어들며, 독립적인 forward solution은 bulk viscosity를 $0.09\%$ 이내로 재현한다.

Abstract

Can a holographic theory be reconstructed from its equation of state? We investigate this question using physics-informed neural networks in the bosonic-mass sector of the Pilch--Warner model. Bondi Four-Momentum Balance in Quantum NSF Classical matching, DKL supermomentum, and quantum energy redistribution

한국어

Bondi evolution은 incoming 및 outgoing News flux를 timelike endpoint momentum과 연관시키며, Dominguez--Kozameh--Ludvigsen radiative sector에서는 이 momentum이 0이 되고 flux가 같아진다. 공통의 radiative Fock domain에서 unitary conjugation이 flux observable을 수송한다. 이들의 classical balance를 추가적인 quantum condition으로 채택하면 exclusive four-momentum conservation이 얻어진다. 또한 그 조건을 부과하지 않고 outgoing energy의 기댓값을 계산한다. incoming energy eigenstate에 대해서는 second-generator commutator가 대각 성분에서 사라져, cubic generator에 의해 결정되는 second-order 계수가 남는다. 직접적인 canonical contraction은 네 개의 external leg 모두와 연결된 기여를 분리해 낸다. equal-energy center-of-mass preparation에서는 axial rotational covariance가 Part~I의 finite helicity-projection prescription 하에서 각도 적분된 exchange 계수를 0으로 만들도록 강제한다. 따라서 connected energy defect는 second order까지 사라진다. spectator를 포함한 전체 기댓값과 더 높은 order의 balance는 미해결로 남아 있다.

Abstract

We derive Bondi four-momentum balance between past and future null infinity in the radiative phase space of the null-surface formulation (NSF). Weyl matching at spatial infinity, together with vanishing endpoint News, gives antipodal continuity of the Bondi mass aspect. Its translation projection is independent of the infrared shear term and transforms as a Lorentz four-vector. Bondi evolution relates the incoming and outgoing News fluxes to the timelike endpoint momenta; in the Dominguez--Kozameh--Ludvigsen radiative sector these momenta vanish and the fluxes are equal. On a common radiative Fock domain, unitary conjugation transports the flux observables. Adopting their classical balance as an additional quantum condition yields exclusive four-momentum conservation. We also calculate the outgoing energy expectation without imposing that condition. For an incoming energy eigenstate, the second-generator commutator vanishes diagonally, leaving a second-order coefficient determined by the cubic generator. Direct canonical contractions isolate the contribution connected to all four external legs. For the equal-energy center-of-mass preparation, axial rotational covariance forces the angularly integrated exchange coefficient to vanish under the finite helicity-projection prescription of Part~I. The connected energy defect therefore vanishes through second order. The full expectation (including spectators) and higher-order balance remain open.

A critical coupling for the magnetic instability of holographic SU(2) plasmas

arXiv:2610.14675 PDF hep-th
Migael Strydom
한국어

우리는 중력이 $SU(2)$ global symmetry를 가진 holographic plasma의 linear magnetic instability를 제거할 수 있음을 보인다. probe limit에서 charged vector boson은 임계 자기장 이상에서 lowest Landau level에서 tachyonic해지며 vortex lattice로 condense한다. AdS$_5$의 Einstein-$SU(2)$ Yang-Mills theory에서는 D'Hoker-Kraus magnetic brane 주위에서 condensate에 대해서만 전개하고 자기장의 backreaction은 정확히 포함한다. 임계 자기장은 coupling $\alpha = \kappa^2/\hat g^2$가 $\alpha_\star = 2/3$에 접근함에 따라 발산하며, 그 이상에서는 normal phase가 어떤 자기장이나 온도에서도 linear charged instability를 갖지 않는다. 이를 영온도에서 보이고 유한온도에서는 수치적으로 확립한다. 영온도에서는 brane의 AdS$_3 \times \mathbb{R}^2$ throat 안에서 lowest Landau level의 mass로부터 자기장이 소거되며, 이 instability는 throat의 Breitenlohner-Freedman bound 위배에 해당한다. onset temperature는 $\alpha_\star$에서 Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless 유형의 essential singularity를 가지며 0이 된다. $d = 4$, 5, 6차원의 two-derivative Einstein-Yang-Mills dual에서 normal phase가 charged instability를 갖는 것은 $C_J/C_T < 8/(d^2(d+1))$일 때, 그리고 그때에 한한다. 이는 $d = 4$에서 $1/10$이다. $\alpha_L = 0.372$ 이상에서는 vortex 사이의 중력적 인력 때문에 uniform lattice가 불안정해지고 transition이 first order가 되므로 $B_c$는 spinodal에 불과하다. $\alpha_\star$ 위에서 condensed phase가 존속하는지는 열린 문제로 남긴다.

Abstract

We show that gravity can remove the linear magnetic instability of a holographic plasma with an $SU(2)$ global symmetry. In the probe limit, charged vector bosons become tachyonic in their lowest Landau level above a critical field and condense into a vortex lattice. Abstract

We study the imaginary part of the holographic timelike entanglement entropy (HTEE) in a charged asymptotically AdS$_5$ black-hole background whose dual field theory exhibits a critical point in the presence of a chemical potential $\mu$ and temperature $T$. Identifying the imaginary part of the HTEE with the imaginary part of the pseudoentropy, we derive an analytical expression for this quantity and investigate its behavior on the stable and unstable thermodynamic branches. We find that the imaginary HTEE vanishes in the uncharged limit and is induced entirely by a nonzero chemical potential, signaling the emergence of quantum coherence between the initial and final states. As the dimensionless ratio $\mu/T$ approaches its critical value, we obtain an inverse-square-root divergence in the derivative of the imaginary HTEE and extract the critical exponent $\theta_I = 1/2$ analytically. We further show that the same exponent governs the temperature response at fixed chemical potential. Our results establish the imaginary part of the HTEE as a non-local quantum-information probe of criticality in strongly coupled systems, and they imply that the initial and final states become maximally coherent at the critical point.

Abstract

We compute the one-loop gauge-field two-point functions analytically in scalar QED on dS$_{d+1}$ for general dimension and scalar mass, using the EAdS reformulation of the in-in formalism arXiv:2007.09993 . The Dirichlet EAdS gauge field acquires a Schwinger-type mass, with its longitudinal mode supplied by a composite of charged scalars obeying opposite boundary conditions, while the Neumann mode remains protected by gauge invariance. In dS, the Dirichlet correction induces an anomalous dimension $\gamma_J$ for the boundary current and modifies the late-time falloff of the electric field. This bulk calculation reproduces the boundary multiplet-recombination result of arXiv:2509.18888 . Summing over the in-in contour branches gives the retarded dS bubble kernel, making direct contact with the Bros-Moschella framework. We establish a spectral Cutkosky rule that expresses the discontinuity of this kernel across the principal-series cut in the Casimir plane in terms of a two-particle cut, with both internal scalar lines placed on shell. Although the full bubble coefficient involves a ${}_7F_6$ hypergeometric function, the cut collapses to a closed product of Gamma functions and determines the complete non-local part of the kernel through a dispersion relation. Analytic continuation of the same cut reproduces the complete one-loop result for $\gamma_J$. In the complex Casimir plane, the loop-corrected Dirichlet photon appears as a pole on the second sheet and is therefore naturally interpreted as a dS resonance, or quasinormal mode, whose frequency is shifted at one loop.
