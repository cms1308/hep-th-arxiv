Comment on "Ultraviolet Completion of the Big Bang in Quadratic Gravity"

arXiv:2609.26827 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Mark A. Shinn
한국어

우리는 Liu, Quintin, Afshordi가 "Ultraviolet Completion of the Big Bang in Quadratic Gravity"에서 제안한 round Euclidean no-boundary saddle을 검토한다. 그들의 대규모 matter sector에 대한 자유롭고 massless한 conformal realization의 경우, 생략된 type-A trace anomaly가 matter multiplicity의 leading order에서 기여한다. one-loop renormalization-group-improved pure quadratic truncation 내에서, sphere effective action과 local trace equation은 모두 running $R^2$ coupling의 stationary point가 total Euler anomaly coefficient가 사라질 때만 round sphere를 지지함을 보여준다. 제안된 beta function을 사용하여, 우리는 나아가 이 coefficient가 $79/420$을 초과할 때 실수 stationary coupling ratio가 존재하지 않음을 발견하는데, 이는 나머지 gravitational contribution이 perturbatively하게 제어되고 order unity일 때 가정된 matter sector에 의해 parametrically하게 위반되는 bound이다. local obstruction은 round half-sphere에도 적용되며 그 radius를 이동시켜도 제거될 수 없다. 이러한 가정 하에서, 제안된 round starting geometry와 그것이 Lorentzian inflation으로 이어진다는 주장은 배제되지만, 다른 initial condition으로부터의 inflation을 배제하지는 않는다.

Abstract

We examine the round Euclidean no-boundary saddle proposed by Liu, Quintin, and Afshordi in "Ultraviolet Completion of the Big Bang in Quadratic Gravity." For a free, massless, conformal realization of their large matter sector, the omitted type-A trace anomaly contributes at leading order in the matter multiplicity. Within the one-loop renormalization-group-improved pure quadratic truncation, both the sphere effective action and the local trace equation show that a stationary point of the running $R^2$ coupling supports a round sphere only if the total Euler anomaly coefficient vanishes. Using the proposed beta function, we further find that no real stationary coupling ratio exists when this coefficient exceeds $79/420$, a bound violated parametrically by the assumed matter sector when the remaining gravitational contributions are perturbatively controlled and of order unity. The local obstruction also applies to a round half-sphere and cannot be removed by shifting its radius. Under these assumptions, the proposed round starting geometry and its stated continuation to Lorentzian inflation are excluded, without ruling out inflation from other initial conditions.

Ordered exponentials and the effective currents of high energy gravity

arXiv:2609.26831 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Sergey Bondarenko
한국어

우리는 Reggeized graviton에 대한 Lipatov type effective action을 유도한다. diffeomorphism invariance로부터 effective current를 order by order로 구성하는 대신, 우리는 light cone을 따라 단일 ordered exponential $W_{ij}$로 이루어진 current를 제안하는데, 이는 한 번 적분된 optical tidal matrix에 의해 생성된다. 이것의 nested ordering은 알려진 perturbative answer를 재현하면서 몇 가지 새로운 항을 추가한다. 이 새로운 항들은 light cone을 따라 non-local하며 diffeomorphism construction이 만들어낼 수 없는 구조를 가진다. third order current의 형태 또한 계산된다. effective action, current to Reggeon vertex, 그리고 current to current interaction은 세 가지 동등한 표현으로, 그 중 하나는 covariant한 형태로 closed form으로 도출된다. 일반적으로 정의된 $W_{ij}$는 ray bundle의 네 가지 매개변수, 즉 그 density, shear, shear의 orientation, 그리고 image의 rotation으로 환원된다. effective current는 이 중 세 가지를 사용하며, 단일 scalar의 second light cone derivative이므로 orientation에는 민감하지 않다. 표준적인 eikonal gravitational Wilson line, 즉 phase와 비교할 때, $W_{ij}$는 동일한 eikonal의 Hessian이다. 그것은 Abelian phase가 갖지 않는 shear와 focusing을 담고 있으며, field에서 first order로 congruence의 Jacobi propagator에 대해 inverse이다. 제안된 ordered operator의 일반화와 gravitational effective action의 응용이 함께 논의된다.

Abstract

We derive a Lipatov type effective action for the Reggeized gravitons. In place of the order by order construction of the effective currents from diffeomorphism invariance, we propose a current which is a single ordered exponential $W_{ij}$ along the light cone, generated by the optical tidal matrix integrated once. The nested ordering of it reproduces the known perturbative answer with some new terms added. These new terms are non-local along the light cone and have a structure which the diffeomorphism construction cannot produce, the form of the third order current is also calculated. The effective action, the current to Reggeon vertex and the current to current interaction follow in closed form, in three equivalent writings, one of them covariant. Generally defined $W_{ij}$ reduces to four parameters of the ray bundle, its density, its shear, the orientation of the shear and a rotation of the image. The effective current uses three of them, being the second light cone derivative of a single scalar, it is not sensitive to the orientation. Comparing with the standard eikonal gravitational Wilson line, which is the phase, $W_{ij}$ is the Hessian of the same eikonal: it carries the shear and the focusing that the Abelian phase does not, and it is inverse to the Jacobi propagator of the congruence at first order in the field. A generalization of the proposed ordered operator is discussed as well as applications of the gravitational effective action.

Collapse to extremality: The third law of black hole thermodynamics in quantum gravity

arXiv:2609.26875 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Gustavo J. Turiaci , Chih-Hung Wu
한국어

우리는 4차원에서 charged black hole에 대한 black hole thermodynamics의 classical third law를 위반하는 two-step construction을 제시한다. $\mathrm{AdS}_2\times X$ throat를 갖는 near-extremal black hole이 먼저 준비되고, 그 다음 charged shell에 의해 유한한 시간 내에 extremality로 몰린다. 이 construction은 두 번째 단계가 near-extremal black hole에 대해 universal함을 시사하며, Kerr black hole에 대한 third law의 위반을 구성하는 데에도 사용될 수 있다. 동일한 description을 통해 우리는 quantum gravity에서 이 과정을 연구할 수 있다. classical collapse condition은 BF-violating matter sector를 선택하며, 이에 대해 우리는 holographic dictionary를 구성하고 quantum transition amplitude를 유도한다. Quantum effect는 classical extremal endpoint를 extremality에서 사라지고 positive energy에서 peak를 이루는 probability distribution으로 대체한다. 이 obstruction은 quantum gravity에서 non-supersymmetric extremal black hole state가 존재하지 않는다는 사실과 일치한다. 우리는 quantum gravity가 중요해질 만큼 peak를 extremality에 충분히 가깝게 몰아가는 데 필요한 preparation time이 black hole entropy에 따라 scaling함을 보인다.

Abstract

We present a two-step construction to violate the classical third law of black hole thermodynamics for charged black holes in four dimensions. A near-extremal black hole with an $\mathrm{AdS}_2\times X$ throat is first prepared and then driven to extremality in finite time by a charged shell. This construction suggests that the second step is universal for near-extremal black holes, and can be used to construct violations of the third law for Kerr black holes. The same description allows us to study the process in quantum gravity. The classical collapse condition selects a BF-violating matter sector, for which we construct a holographic dictionary and derive the quantum transition amplitude. Quantum effects replace the classical extremal endpoint by a probability distribution that vanishes at extremality and peaks at positive energy. This obstruction is consistent with the absence of non-supersymmetric extremal black hole states in quantum gravity. We show that driving the peak close enough to extremality for quantum gravity to become important requires a preparation time that scales with the black hole entropy.

Asymptotic limits in class-$\mathcal{S}$ theories and non-Abelian Hodge theory

arXiv:2609.26883 PDF hep-th
Thomas W. Grimm , Amineh Mohseni
한국어

우리는 type-$A$ class-$\mathcal S$ theory에서 asymptotic limit에 대한 non-Abelian Hodge-theoretic refinement를 전개하며, degenerating UV curve의 geometric description을 관련된 Hitchin system에 의해 인코딩된 gauge-theoretic data로 풍부하게 만든다. UV curve의 degeneration은 긴 plumbing tube를 생성하는데, 우리는 이를 regular-singular logarithmic model을 허용하는 Hitchin-Simpson flat connection의 local monodromy data로 갖춘다. monodromy의 semisimple 부분과 unipotent 부분은 각각 tube를 통과하는 flat section의 power-law growth와 logarithmic growth를 지배한다. 이 non-Abelian holonomy를 geometric Picard-Lefschetz monodromy와 결합하면 Higgs-bundle information을 포함하고 boundary divisor의 intersection까지 tube-wise로 확장되는 decorated cusp label을 얻는다. 각 tube에 대해, 우리는 fixture와 gluing data에 의해 지정된 weak gauge algebra가 reductive monodromy centralizer 안에 놓일 것을 요구한다. type $A$에서, 우리는 local monodromy label을 semisimple 부분의 eigenspace multiplicity와 unipotent 부분의 nilpotent logarithm의 Jordan type에 의해 지정된 discrete type으로 조직화한다. 마지막으로, 우리는 four-punctured sphere 위의 $SL(3,\mathbb C)$와 two-punctured torus 위의 $SL(4,\mathbb C)$에 대해 이 construction을 예시한다.

Abstract

We develop a non-Abelian Hodge-theoretic refinement of asymptotic limits in type-$A$ class-$\mathcal S$ theories, enriching the geometric description of the degenerating UV curve with gauge-theoretic data encoded by the associated Hitchin system. A degeneration of the UV curve produces a long plumbing tube, which we equip with the local monodromy data of a Hitchin-Simpson flat connection admitting a regular-singular logarithmic model. The semisimple and unipotent parts of the monodromy govern, respectively, the power-law and logarithmic growth of flat sections through the tube. Combining this non-Abelian holonomy with the geometric Picard-Lefschetz monodromy yields a decorated cusp label that incorporates Higgs-bundle information and extends tube-wise to intersections of boundary divisors. For each tube, we require the weak gauge algebra specified by the fixture and gluing data to lie in the reductive monodromy centralizer. In type $A$, we organize the local monodromy labels into discrete types specified by the eigenspace multiplicities of the semisimple part and the Jordan type of the nilpotent logarithm of the unipotent part. Finally, we illustrate the construction for $SL(3,\mathbb C)$ on the four-punctured sphere and $SL(4,\mathbb C)$ on the two-punctured torus.

Maximal Total Quantum Dimension at Bounded Rank in Dijkgraaf-Witten Theories

arXiv:2609.26914 PDF hep-thcond-mat.str-elmath.QA
Ce Shen
한국어

우리는 3차원 Dijkgraaf--Witten theory에서 bounded categorical rank cutoff $R$에서의 maximal total quantum dimension을 연구한다. 이 optimization problem은 물리적으로 고정된 torus ground-state degeneracy에서 maximal state-optimized torus topological entanglement entropy를 지배하며, 동시에 이러한 topological order phase를 분류하는 데 필요한 search space에 대한 정량적 제약을 제공한다. 우리는 topological twist (3-cocycle)와 gauge-group structure가 어떻게 독립적으로 이 trade-off를 지배하는지 분석한다. 고정된 소수 $p$에 대한 elementary-Abelian gauge group의 경우, 우리는 cocycle twist를 도입하면 maximum total quantum dimension이 rank cutoff에서 square-root growth $\Theta_p(R^{1/2})$에서 linear growth $\Theta_p(R)$로 상승함을 증명하는데, 이는 cyclic holonomy의 geometric protection에 의해 주도된다. untwisted theory의 경우, 우리는 symmetric group과 alternating group에 대해 sharp envelope를 확립하고, almost-simple, solvable, radical-free family 전반에 걸쳐 group-theoretic bound를 유도한다. 특히, almost-simple group과 그 direct product는 quadratic-logarithmic upper envelope, 즉 $\log\mathcal{D} = O((\log R)^2)$를 따른다. 마지막으로, 우리는 이 scale들을 WZW model과 비교하고 일반적인 quadratic-logarithmic envelope conjecture의 상태를 논의한다.

Abstract

We investigate the maximal total quantum dimension at a bounded categorical rank cutoff $R$ in three-dimensional Dijkgraaf--Witten theories. This optimization problem physically governs the maximal state-optimized torus topological entanglement entropy at a fixed torus ground-state degeneracy, while providing a quantitative constraint on the search space required to classify these topological order phases. We analyze how topological twists (3-cocycles) and gauge-group structures independently govern this trade-off. For elementary-Abelian gauge groups at a fixed prime $p$, we prove that introducing cocycle twists elevates the maximum total quantum dimension from square-root growth $\Theta_p(R^{1/2})$ to linear growth $\Theta_p(R)$ in the rank cutoff, driven by the geometric protection of cyclic holonomies. For untwisted theories, we establish sharp envelopes for symmetric and alternating gauge groups, and derive group-theoretic bounds across almost-simple, solvable, and radical-free families. In particular, almost-simple groups and their direct products obey a quadratic-logarithmic upper envelope: $\log\mathcal{D} = O((\log R)^2)$. Finally, we compare these scales against WZW models and discuss the status of the general quadratic-logarithmic envelope conjecture.

A note on gauge-covariant vertex operators of the ambitwistor string

arXiv:2609.27026 PDF hep-th
Osvaldo Chandia
한국어

우리는 bosonic ambitwistor string의 antighost-independent subcomplex 안에서 gauge-covariant unintegrated vertex operator를 구성한다. linearized gauge field에 대해, world-sheet ghost-derivative term이 표준 vertex를 완성하여 BRST closure가 Lorenz condition 없이 $\p^nF_{nmI}=0$을 부과하도록 한다. rank-two sector에서 우리는 diffeomorphism-invariant spin-two subsector를 분리하는 데 충분한 antighost-free ansatz를 사용한다. auxiliary field를 제거하고 antisymmetric 및 scalar combination을 일관되게 0으로 설정한 후, BRST closure는 transversality를 부과하지 않고 $R_{mn}=0$을 준다. 우리는 이 construction을 bosonic ambitwistor string의 full relative BRST cohomology와 구별하는데, 후자의 추가적인 antighost-dependent state들은 알려진 higher-derivative spectrum으로 이어진다.

Abstract

We construct gauge-covariant unintegrated vertex operators in an antighost-independent subcomplex of the bosonic ambitwistor string. For a linearized gauge field, a world-sheet ghost-derivative term completes the standard vertex so that BRST closure imposes $\p^nF_{nmI}=0$ without the Lorenz condition. In the rank-two sector we use an antighost-free ansatz that is sufficient to isolate a diffeomorphism-invariant spin-two subsector. After eliminating auxiliary fields and consistently setting the antisymmetric and scalar combinations to zero, BRST closure gives $R_{mn}=0$ without imposing transversality. We distinguish this construction from the full relative BRST cohomology of the bosonic ambitwistor string, whose additional antighost-dependent states lead to the known higher-derivative spectrum.

Spectral Fractional Bosonic Strings: Exact Polyakov Measure and the Critical Dimension

arXiv:2609.27873 PDF hep-thmath-ph
Tianhao Wu , Gabriele La Nave
한국어

Fractional Laplacian은 quantum matter와 holographic boundary system에서 anomalous scaling과 covariant nonlocal response를 인코딩한다. 우리는 대응하는 scalar spectral theory를 dynamical closed worldsheet 위에 놓는다. metric-dependent intertwiner는 fractional quadratic form을 $D$개의 free boson의 quadratic form으로 사상하며, 그 zeta Jacobian은 matter determinant weight를 $D$에서 $sD$로 바꾼다. prime-determinant transformation은 zero-mode area factor를 포함하며 moduli density와 Weyl coefficient $c_{\rm det}=sD$를 모두 결정한다. 따라서 Weyl cancellation은 $D_\ast=26/s$를 선택한다. integer critical locus $1\leq D\leq26$에서, $N=26-D$개의 spectator boson은 residual determinant를 local critical CFT로 바꾼다. 그 fixed-area genus-$g$ vacuum measure는 26개의 free boson의 그것이며, 그 torus trace는 modular invariant하고, 그 plumbing channel은 state resolved되며, 그 BRST charge는 nilpotent하다. $D$개의 distinguished coordinate는 spectral composite로 남으며, 그 pullback은 Koba--Nielsen amplitude와 massless vertex condition을 산출한다. 이 distinguished sector에 결합된 target background는 보통의 one-loop tensor beta function을 유지하며, dilaton deficit $sD-26$과 그에 대응하는 string-frame action을 갖는다. 따라서 worldsheet gravity는 fractional exponent를 quantum-geometric consistency parameter로 바꾼다.

Abstract

Fractional Laplacians encode anomalous scaling and covariant nonlocal response in quantum matter and holographic boundary systems. We place the corresponding scalar spectral theory on a dynamical closed worldsheet. A metric-dependent intertwiner maps the fractional quadratic form to that of $D$ free bosons, while its zeta Jacobian changes the matter determinant weight from $D$ to $sD$. The prime-determinant transformation includes the zero-mode area factor and determines both the moduli density and the Weyl coefficient $c_{\rm det}=sD$. Weyl cancellation therefore selects $D_\ast=26/s$. On the integer critical locus $1\leq D\leq26$, $N=26-D$ spectator bosons turn the residual determinant into a local critical CFT. Its fixed-area genus-$g$ vacuum measure is that of 26 free bosons, its torus trace is modular invariant, its plumbing channels are state resolved, and its BRST charge is nilpotent. The $D$ distinguished coordinates remain spectral composites; their pullback yields the Koba--Nielsen amplitudes and massless vertex conditions. Target backgrounds coupled to this distinguished sector retain the ordinary one-loop tensor beta functions, with dilaton deficit $sD-26$ and the corresponding string-frame action. Worldsheet gravity thus turns the fractional exponent into a quantum-geometric consistency parameter.

Relational background field formulation of discrete quantum gravity models: The case of $O(N)$ vector models in zero dimensions

arXiv:2609.28032 PDF hep-thcond-mat.stat-mech
Vincent Lahoche , Dine Ousmane Samary , Parham Radpay
한국어

최근 논문 arxiv.org/abs/2609.21031 에서, 우리는 discrete quantum gravity model에 대한 renormalization group (RG)의 새로운 접근법으로서 relational background field의 개념을 도입했으며, random matrix model에 초점을 맞추었다. 제안된 방법은 모델의 gauge symmetry (전형적으로 $U(N)$ 또는 $O(N)$)를 보존한다는 이중의 이점을 제공하는데, 이 대칭성의 깨짐은 기존 접근법에서 미해결 문제로 남아 있었으며, 동시에 continuum 또는 infrared (IR) limit에 대한 더 만족스러운 처리를 제공한다. 이 limit에서, theory는 dimension $D = 2k + 3$의 non-local field theory로 기술되며, 여기서 $k$는 random matrix theory에 의해 결정되는 effective spectrum의 cut의 개수를 나타낸다. 이 pedagogical paper는 $O(N)$을 symmetry group으로 하는 zero dimension의 real random vector model에 대해 이 construction을 review한다. background-field-type formulation의 일반적인 전략을 따라, 우리는 BBP (Baik-Ben Arrous-P\'ech\'e) phase transition을 사용하여, matrix-valued intermediate field를 포함하는 quartic interaction의 partial Hubbard--Stratonovich decomposition이 scale에 대한 선호되는 개념이 나타나도록 함을 보인다. 이 scale은 다시 RG flow를 정의하며, 그 perturbative structure가 분석된다. 우리의 계산은 \textit{asymptotic} Wilson--Fisher-type fixed point의 존재를 보이며, 그 유일한 relevant critical exponent는 이러한 model들의 double-scaling critical exponent와 정성적으로 일치한다.

Abstract

In a recent paper arxiv.org/abs/2609.21031 , we have introduced the concept of a relational background field as a new approach to the renormalization group (RG) for discrete quantum gravity models, focusing on random matrix models. The proposed method offers the dual advantage of preserving the model's gauge symmetry (typically $U(N)$ or $O(N)$), whose breaking in conventional approaches has remained an open problem, and providing a more satisfactory treatment of the continuum or infrared (IR) limit. In this limit, the theory is described by a non-local field theory in dimension $D = 2k + 3$, where $k$ denotes the number of cuts in the effective spectrum as dictated by random matrix theory. This pedagogical paper reviews this construction for real random vector models in zero dimensions, with $O(N)$ as the symmetry group. Following the general strategy of background-field-type formulations, we show, using the BBP (Baik-Ben Arrous-P'ech'e) phase transition, that a partial Hubbard--Stratonovich decomposition of the quartic interaction, involving a matrix-valued intermediate field, allows a preferred notion of scale to emerge. This scale, in turn, defines an RG flow, whose perturbative structure is analyzed. Our calculation demonstrates the existence of an \textit{asymptotic} Wilson--Fisher-type fixed point, whose sole relevant critical exponent is in qualitative agreement with the double-scaling critical exponent of these models.

Equal-GNO Sectors and Fuzzy-Sphere Vacua: Large-Charge ADHM-BMN Index Matching

arXiv:2609.28119 PDF hep-th
Sarthak Duary , Kangning Liu
한국어

우리는 3차원 ADHM theory의 superconformal index와 BMN matrix quantum mechanics의 fixed-vacuum Witten index 사이의 protected-sector relation을 확립한다. 이 비교는 ADHM localization formula의 positive equal-magnetic-charge sector와 동일한 fuzzy-sphere block으로 이루어진 BMN vacuum을 선택한다. equal magnetic charge는 complete multiplicity gauge group을 보존하며 vector determinant와 adjoint determinant를 공통 charge와 무관하게 만든다. 반면 fundamental field는 monopole background 전체를 경험하며 magnetic gap을 발전시킨다. large-GNO-charge limit에서, 이들의 oscillator determinant는 protected expansion의 모든 fixed order에서 decouple하는 반면, 연관된 vacuum monomial은 살아남아 sector의 normalization을 고정한다. 이 normalization 이후, large-GNO-charge limit에서의 ADHM matrix integral은 BMN index의 large-block limit과 계수 단위로 일치한다. 따라서 이 matching은 두 가지 물리적으로 구별되는 메커니즘으로부터 발생한다. large-GNO-charge limit에서는 fundamental-hypermultiplet excitation이 magnetically heavy해져서 ADHM side에서 decouple하는 반면, large-block limit에서는 finite fuzzy-sphere angular-momentum cutoff가 BMN side에서 제거되어 complete angular tower가 남는다. 우리는 이 대응을 계수 단위로 확립하고, exact determinant identity를 large-charge asymptotics와 구별하며, M-theory uplift 이후 GNO-charge partition이 BMN fuzzy-sphere block들 사이의 longitudinal momentum의 distribution이 됨을 보인다.

Abstract

We establish a protected-sector relation between the superconformal index of the three-dimensional ADHM theory and the fixed-vacuum Witten index of BMN matrix quantum mechanics. The comparison selects a positive equal-magnetic-charge sector of the ADHM localization formula and a BMN vacuum made from identical fuzzy-sphere blocks. Equal magnetic charges preserve the complete multiplicity gauge group and make the vector and adjoint determinants independent of the common charge. Fundamental fields instead experience the full monopole background and develop a magnetic gap. In the large-GNO-charge limit, their oscillator determinant decouples from every fixed order of the protected expansion, while the associated vacuum monomial survives and fixes the normalization of the sector. After this normalization, the ADHM matrix integral in the large-GNO-charge limit agrees coefficientwise with the large-block limit of the BMN index. The matching therefore arises from two physically distinct mechanisms: in the large-GNO-charge limit, the fundamental-hypermultiplet excitations become magnetically heavy and decouple on the ADHM side, while in the large-block limit, the finite fuzzy-sphere angular-momentum cutoff is removed on the BMN side, leaving the complete angular tower. We establish the correspondence coefficientwise, distinguish the exact determinant identities from the large-charge asymptotics, and show that the GNO-charge partition becomes, after the M-theory uplift, the distribution of longitudinal momentum among the BMN fuzzy-sphere blocks.

Geometries of Quantum Field Theories

arXiv:2609.28210 PDF hep-thmath-phmath.GTmath.QA
Masahito Yamazaki
한국어

이 책은 2015년 Saiensu-sha에서 일본어로 출판된 책의 영어 번역본이다. 이 책은 3d-3d correspondence, 즉 3d $\mathcal{N}=2$ supersymmetric gauge theory와 3-manifold의 geometry 사이의 관계에 대한 입문서로서, 이는 6d $\mathcal{N}=(2,0)$ theory의 compactification으로부터 발생한다. 서술 방식은 top-down이 아니라 bottom-up이다. 즉 6d theory에서 시작하는 대신, 우리는 quantum field theory란 무엇인가를 묻는 것에서 시작하여 geometry가 스스로 드러나도록 한다. 그 과정에서 우리는 renormalization과 low-energy effective theory, field theory들을 결합하는 연산으로서의 gauging, 그로부터 생겨나는 3d theory에 대한 $Sp(2n, \mathbb{Z})$-action, 3d $\mathcal{N}=2$ supersymmetric theory와 그 duality, squashed three-sphere partition function과 supersymmetric localization, 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ theory의 duality domain wall, quantum Teichmuller theory, complex Chern-Simons theory 그리고 3-manifold 위의 flat $SL(2, \mathbb{C})$-connection의 moduli space의 quantization을 논의하며, quantum dilogarithm function이 반복되는 thread로 등장한다. 전체적인 강조점은 단일 field theory에서는 보이지 않고 quantum field theory들의 theory space에서만 나타나는 geometric structure와 algebraic structure에 있다. 부록은 다양한 차원에서의 supersymmetry, classical 및 quantum dilogarithm function, 그리고 cluster algebra를 요약한다. 각 chapter에는 난이도가 표시된 연습문제가 포함되어 있다.

Abstract

This is an English translation of a book originally published in Japanese by Saiensu-sha in 2015. The book is an introduction to the 3d-3d correspondence, the relation between 3d $\mathcal{N}=2$ supersymmetric gauge theories and the geometry of 3-manifolds, which arises from the compactification of the 6d $\mathcal{N}=(2,0)$ theory. The presentation is bottom-up rather than top-down: instead of starting from the 6d theory, we begin by asking what a quantum field theory is, and let the geometry emerge on its own. Along the way we discuss renormalization and low-energy effective theories, gauging as an operation which glues field theories together, the resulting $Sp(2n, \mathbb{Z})$-action on 3d theories, 3d $\mathcal{N}=2$ supersymmetric theories and their dualities, the squashed three-sphere partition function and supersymmetric localization, duality domain walls of 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ theories, quantum Teichmuller theory, complex Chern-Simons theory and the quantization of the moduli space of flat $SL(2, \mathbb{C})$-connections on 3-manifolds, with quantum dilogarithm functions as a recurring thread. The emphasis throughout is on geometric and algebraic structures which are invisible in a single field theory, and appear only in the theory space of quantum field theories. Appendices summarize supersymmetry in various dimensions, classical and quantum dilogarithm functions, and cluster algebras. Exercises with difficulty ratings are included in each chapter.

Fermionic cosmic strings in dynamical Chern-Simons gravity

arXiv:2609.28421 PDF hep-thastro-ph.COgr-qchep-ph
Stephon Alexander , Heliudson Bernardo , Shengyuan Zhang
한국어

우리는 dynamical Chern-Simons (dCS) gravity의 fermionic ultraviolet completion에서 chiral phase transition에 의해 생성되는 topological defect를 연구한다. 이 모델은 fermion에 대한 chiral Yukawa coupling을 갖는 complex scalar field를 포함한다. chiral symmetry breaking scale 아래에서, gapped bulk mode를 integrating out하면 gravitational Chern-Simons coupling이 생성되며, 우리는 이것이 phase transition으로부터의 cosmic string solution을 수정한다고 주장한다. 결과적인 corrected string metric은 Callan-Harvey fermionic zero mode solution의 일반화에 의해 지지되는데, 여기서 이 zero mode들이 생성하는 null current는 parity violation의 메커니즘을 제공한다. weak-coupling 및 weak-gravity regime에서, 우리는 이 exterior metric에 대한 leading order-reduced dCS correction을 유도한다. 우리는 또한 dCS gravity에서 generic string configuration에 대한 effective action description을 논의한다. gravitational Pontryagin term은 chiral worldsheet theory의 gravitational anomaly를 상쇄하는 데 필요한 anomaly inflow를 제공한다.

Abstract

We investigate topological defects produced by a chiral phase transition in a fermionic ultraviolet completion of dynamical Chern-Simons (dCS) gravity. The model contains a complex scalar field with a chiral Yukawa coupling to fermions. Below the chiral symmetry breaking scale, integrating out the gapped bulk modes produces a gravitational Chern-Simons coupling which, we argue, modifies cosmic string solutions from the phase transition. The resulting corrected string metric is supported by a generalization of the Callan-Harvey fermionic zero mode solution, in which the null current generated by these zero modes provides a mechanism for parity violation. In the weak-coupling and weak-gravity regime, we derive the leading order-reduced dCS correction to this exterior metric. We also discuss the effective action description for generic string configurations in dCS gravity. The gravitational Pontryagin term provides the anomaly inflow required to cancel the gravitational anomaly of the chiral worldsheet theory.

Scalar Gauss-Bonnet Wormholes and the Imaginary Distance Bound

arXiv:2609.28456 PDF hep-thgr-qchep-ph
Alex Kehagias , Antonio Riotto
한국어

우리는 Gauss-Bonnet invariant에 linearly coupling된 massless scalar를 갖는 4차원 gravity에서 $O(4)$ symmetric Euclidean wormhole을 연구한다. nonzero coupling에서, Einstein wormhole은 finite coupling에서 그 branch가 끝날 때까지 Kontsevich-Segal-Witten criterion을 만족하는 regular complex saddle이 된다. asymptotically flat space에서, Gauss-Bonnet term은 finite wormhole에 대한 scalar displacement와 on-shell action을 수정하지만, 두 correction의 size dependent한 부분은 large throat limit에서 사라진다. 따라서 original imaginary distance threshold는 변하지 않는다. negative cosmological constant는 이미 Einstein large throat imaginary distance를 그 flat space 값에 비해 낮춘다. Gauss-Bonnet coupling도 존재할 때, scalar는 더 이상 modulus가 아닌데, 이는 logarithmic running을 발전시키고 그 trajectory가 무한한 길이를 갖기 때문이다. logarithmic asymptotic behavior를 빼면 finite한 regulated displacement가 남는다. 그 large throat 값은 Einstein AdS 결과로부터 Gauss-Bonnet interaction에 의해 위쪽으로 이동하며, renormalized fixed charge action의 leading large charge behavior를 결정한다.

Abstract

We study $O(4)$ symmetric Euclidean wormholes in four-dimensional gravity with a massless scalar linearly coupled to the Gauss-Bonnet invariant. At nonzero coupling, the Einstein wormhole becomes a regular complex saddle that satisfies the Kontsevich-Segal-Witten criterion until its branch ends at a finite coupling. In asymptotically flat space, the Gauss-Bonnet term modifies the scalar displacement and the on-shell action for finite wormholes, but the size dependent parts of both corrections vanish in the large throat limit. The original imaginary distance threshold therefore remains unchanged. A negative cosmological constant already lowers the Einstein large throat imaginary distance relative to its flat space value. When the Gauss-Bonnet coupling is also present, the scalar is no longer a modulus as it develops a logarithmic running and its trajectory has infinite length. Subtracting the logarithmic asymptotic behavior leaves a finite regulated displacement. Its large throat value is shifted upward from the Einstein AdS result by the Gauss-Bonnet interaction and determines the leading large charge behavior of the renormalized fixed charge action.
