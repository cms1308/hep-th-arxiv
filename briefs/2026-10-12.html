Lattice Monte Carlo of $λϕ^4$ on Euclidean de Sitter

arXiv:2610.12547 PDF hep-thgr-qchep-lat
Angelo Caravano
한국어

우리는 Euclidean de Sitter space, $S^4$ 위에서 수행된 quantum field theory의 첫 Monte Carlo simulation을 보고한다. 공간을 discretize하는 대신, 우리는 spherical harmonics에 기반한 spectral lattice를 사용하여 유한한 cutoff에서 모든 symmetry를 정확하게 유지한다. massless $\lambda\phi^4$ theory에 대해, zero-mode distribution은 weak coupling에서 Starobinsky-Yokoyama equilibrium을 재현하며, 그 편차는 $\lambda$의 one-loop renormalization을 밀접하게 따른다. 강한 bare coupling에서는, renormalized quartic coupling이 one-loop renormalization-group flow의 상한 근처에서 saturation된다. 이러한 saturation은 flat space에서 Higgs mass를 제한하는 triviality mechanism의 de Sitter 대응물이다. 우리의 결과는 de Sitter에서 light scalar들의 infrared divergence가 stochastic formalism에 의해 올바르게 resum됨을 비섭동적으로(nonperturbatively) 확인한다. 더 넓게는, 이 연구는 de Sitter space를 lattice Monte Carlo 방법에 개방한다.

Abstract

We report the first Monte Carlo simulation of quantum field theory on Euclidean de Sitter space, $S^4$. Rather than discretizing space, we use a spectral lattice based on spherical harmonics, keeping all symmetries exact at finite cutoff. For a massless $\lambda\phi^4$ theory, the zero-mode distribution reproduces the Starobinsky-Yokoyama equilibrium at weak coupling, and deviations closely follow the one-loop renormalization of $\lambda$. At strong bare coupling, the renormalized quartic coupling saturates near the ceiling of the one-loop renormalization-group flow. This saturation is the de Sitter counterpart of the triviality mechanism that bounds the Higgs mass in flat space. Our results confirm nonperturbatively that the infrared divergences of light scalars in de Sitter are correctly resummed by the stochastic formalism. More broadly, this work opens up de Sitter space to lattice Monte Carlo methods.

Proving the OSV conjecture with the gravitational path integral

arXiv:2610.12550 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Nikolay Bobev , Jan Boruch , Luca V. Iliesiu , Dawid Maskalaniec
한국어

OSV conjecture는 Type IIA string theory의 Calabi-Yau compactification을 통해 얻어진 $4d$ $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity에서의 black hole들의 helicity supertrace를, 주어진 magnetic black hole charge들과 electric chemical potential들의 집합에 대해 moduli space 내 attractor point에서 평가된 topological string partition function의 modulus 제곱과 관련짓는다. 우리는 이 index를 4d supergravity derivative expansion의 모든 order까지 계산하는 Euclidean black hole saddle들을 찾아내고, equivariant localization을 이용하여 그 action을 계산한다. gravitational on-shell action은 각 black hole의 북극과 남극으로 완전히 localize되며, 두 contribution은 각각 moduli의 attractor 값에서 평가된 holomorphic 및 anti-holomorphic topological string free energy에 의해 결정된다. single-center black hole saddle의 경우, 이는 topological string coupling의 모든 order에서 OSV conjecture의 증명을 제공한다. 동일한 localization 공식은 multi-center black hole과 같이 helicity supertrace에 contribution하는 모든 saddle에도 적용되며, 이는 conjecture의 비섭동적(non-perturbative) refinement로 이어진다.

Abstract

The OSV conjecture relates the helicity supertrace of black holes in $4d$ $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity obtained through a Calabi--Yau compactification of Type IIA string theory to the modulus squared of the topological string partition function evaluated at the attractor point in moduli space for a given set of magnetic black hole charges and electric chemical potentials. We find Euclidean black hole saddles that compute this index to all orders in the 4d supergravity derivative expansion and calculate their action using equivariant localization. The gravitational on-shell action localizes entirely to the north and south poles of each black hole, with the two contributions determined by the holomorphic and anti-holomorphic topological string free energies, respectively, evaluated at the attractor values of the moduli. For single-center black hole saddles, this provides a proof of the OSV conjecture to all orders in the topological string coupling. The same localization formula also applies to all saddles that contribute to the helicity supertrace, such as multi-center black holes, which leads to a non-perturbative refinement of the conjecture.

GLSMs for non-polynomial deformations and generalized complete intersection Calabi-Yau manifolds

Abstract

Gauged Linear Sigma Models (GLSMs) have long been a key tool in understanding Calabi-Yau compactifications of string theory. Likewise, the somewhat more exotic generalized complete intersection Calabi-Yau construction has resisted a GLSM description. In this paper we resolve both of these omissions using completely standard GLSMs with polynomial superpotentials. The non-polynomial structure seen in the geometric constructions only arises in the associated GLSM upon restriction to the vacuum space of the theory. This feature enables the construction to avoid singularities in field space that could have arisen if non-polynomial structure were incorporated directly into the GLSM superpotential.

Quantum limit to transparency

arXiv:2610.12560 PDF hep-thcond-mat.otherhep-ph
Angelo Esposito , Alberto Nicolis , Federico Piazza
한국어

우리는 어떤 material도 nontrivial한 index of refraction을 갖는 한 빛에 대해 완전히 transparent하게 만들어질 수 없다고 주장한다. photon이 phonon으로 quantum decay하는 것은 제거할 수 없는(ineliminable) attenuation의 원천이다. 이는 다른 모든 excitation들이 gap을 가지게 되고, impurity들이 제거되며, 온도가 0으로 설정된 이후에도 여전히 남는다. 단순함을 위해 superfluid의 경우로 제한하여, 우리는 effective field theory 방법을 통해 index of refraction의 imaginary part에 대한 해당 low-frequency contribution을 결정한다. 이는 $\omega^4$로 scale하며 $\omega \rightarrow 0$에서 real part의 nonanalyticity를 유도한다. 우리는 또한 laser의 전자기장과 같은 large coherent electromagnetic field로 결과를 확장하여, 그 효과가 강한 nonlinear enhancement를 받을 수 있음을 보인다. 우리의 결과는 매질(medium)의 transparency에 대한 진정한 quantum lower bound이다.

Abstract

We argue that no material can be made completely transparent to light as long as it has a nontrivial index of refraction. The quantum decay of photons into phonons is an ineliminable source of attenuation. This survives even once all other excitations have been gapped away, impurities have been removed, and the temperature has been set to zero. Restricting for simplicity to the case of a superfluid, we determine via effective field theory methods the corresponding low-frequency contribution to the imaginary part of the index of refraction. This scales as $\omega^4$ and induces a nonanalyticity of the real part for $\omega \rightarrow 0$. We also extend our result to large coherent electromagnetic fields, such as those of a laser, showing that the effect can receive strong nonlinear enhancements. Our result is a genuine quantum lower bound to the transparency of a medium.

The Real EFThedron: A Moment Geometry for the Nonperturbative S-Matrix

arXiv:2610.12615 PDF hep-th
Laurentiu Rodina , Anna Tokareva , Yongjun Xu
한국어

S-matrix bootstrap은 unitarity, analyticity, crossing symmetry를 통해 low-energy interaction을 제한한다. effective field theory coupling에 대해, positivity bound는 자연스러운 geometric formulation인 EFThedron을 가지며, 여기서 허용된 coupling들은 classical positive moment problem을 형성한다. 우리는 이 geometry가 완전한 nonlinear unitarity로 확장됨을 보인다. amplitude에 energy의 fractional power를 곱하면 그 real part 또한 moment가 되어, unitarity가 bounded complex moment problem이 된다. analyticity는 crossing symmetry에 의해 부과되는 null constraint들과 유사하게, 동일한 coupling들을 재구성하기 위해 서로 다른 kernel들을 요구하는 일련의 linear constraint가 된다. 2차원에서는 이 문제가 정확히 풀릴 수 있으며, moment problem의 extremal measure로서 Castillejo--Dalitz--Dyson amplitude로 이어진다. 4차원에서는 massless EFThedron model과 massive scalar scattering에 대한 closed-form 및 semi-analytic bound를 얻는다. massive scalar의 경우, analyticity constraint는 기존에 발표된 dual bound를 개선하여 primal region과의 간극을 줄인다. amplitude의 real part와 imaginary part를 동등한 위치에 둠으로써, Real EFThedron은 unitarity, analyticity, crossing을 결합하는 강력한 framework를 제공하며, consistent한 quantum field theory들의 공간을 지도화하는 새로운 경로를 연다.

Abstract

The S-matrix bootstrap restricts low-energy interactions through unitarity, analyticity and crossing symmetry. For effective field theory couplings, positivity bounds have a natural geometric formulation, the EFThedron, in which the allowed couplings form the classical positive moment problem. We show that this geometry extends to full nonlinear unitarity. Multiplying the amplitude by fractional powers of the energy turns its real part into a moment as well, so that unitarity becomes a bounded complex moment problem. Analyticity becomes a set of linear constraints requiring different kernels to reconstruct the same couplings, analogous to the null constraints imposed by crossing symmetry. In two dimensions the problem can be solved exactly, leading to Castillejo--Dalitz--Dyson amplitudes as extremal measures of the moment problem. In four dimensions we obtain closed-form and semi-analytic bounds for the massless EFThedron model and for massive scalar scattering. For massive scalars, the analyticity constraints improve on published dual bounds, reducing the gap to the primal region. By placing the real and imaginary parts of amplitudes on an equal footing, the Real EFThedron provides a powerful framework combining unitarity, analyticity and crossing, opening a new route to mapping the space of consistent quantum field theories.

Abstract

A tropical quantum field theory is an exactly solvable deformation limit of an ordinary QFT, governed by a non-linear partial differential equation. We use the integrable two-dimensional sine-Gordon theory as a testbench to compare the exact features of an undeformed model with its tropical counterpart. We classify scale-invariant tropical QFTs in $D=2$ and leverage functional renormalization group methods to demonstrate that all scale-invariant theories with periodic effective actions are infrared fixed points of flows originating from sine-Gordon Lagrangians. For the tropical sine-Gordon model, we compute the $\beta$-functions exactly, solve the renormalized theory both via a convergent expansion and numerically for all admissible couplings, and illustrate the complete renormalization group flow from the Gaussian to the IR fixed point. Finally, we study the large-order behaviour of the loop expansion and relate it to the Hamilton-Jacobi equation, whose solutions we interpret as tropical instantons.

우리는 콤팩트 쌍곡 리만 표면(compact hyperbolic Riemann surface) $\Sigma_2$에 감긴 M5-branes로부터 발생하는 4차원 $\mathcal{N}=1$ class $\mathcal{S}$ superconformal field theories에서 가벼운 연산자(light operator) 스펙트럼의 보편적 부문(universal sector)을 규명한다. 우리는 large $N$에서 작업하며, twist parameter $z$의 일반적인 값들에 대해 dual Bah--Beem--Bobev--Wecht anti-de Sitter backgrounds 주위의 Kaluza--Klein fluctuations을 분석한다. 이러한 스펙트럼을 얻기 위해, 우리는 적절한 $z$-dependent generalised $G$-structure를 부과하여 최근 유도된 mass operators를 $\Sigma_2$ 위에서 상수인 모드들로 특수화한다. 그런 다음 우리는 결과로 얻어진 eigenstates를 명확한 flavour quantum numbers를 갖는 long and protected $\mathcal{N}=1$ supermultiplets로 조직한다. $z$가 변함에 따라, 스펙트럼에서 isolated multiplets와 multiplets의 infinite towers를 모두 포함하는 풍부한 패턴들이 나타나며, 우리는 이를 해석적으로(analytically) 특성화한다.

Abstract

We determine a universal sector of the spectrum of light operators in four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=1$ class $\mathcal{S}$ superconformal field theories arising from M5-branes wrapped on a compact hyperbolic Riemann surface $\Sigma_2$. We work at large $N$, analysing Kaluza--Klein fluctuations about the dual Bah--Beem--Bobev--Wecht anti-de Sitter backgrounds for general values of the twist parameter $z$. To obtain these spectra, we specialise recently derived mass operators to modes that are constant on $\Sigma_2$ by imposing a suitable $z$-dependent generalised $G$-structure. We then organise the resulting eigenstates into long and protected $\mathcal{N}=1$ supermultiplets with definite flavour quantum numbers. As $z$ varies, rich patterns emerge in the spectrum, including both isolated multiplets and infinite towers of multiplets, which we characterise analytically.
