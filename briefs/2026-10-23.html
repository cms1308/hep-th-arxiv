Twisted scattering from Chern-Simons theory

arXiv:2610.30232 PDF hep-thcond-mat.str-el
Guillermo Arias-Tamargo , Philip Boyle Smith , Rishi Mouland , Max Velásquez Cotini Hutt
한국어

우리는 좌측 및 우측 운동 성분에 서로 다르게 작용하는 비이상(non-anomalous) 연속 대칭을 보존하는 2차원 Dirac fermion의 경계 조건을 고려한다. 이러한 경계는 해당 대칭을 보존하는 Weyl fermion의 topological defect와 동등하다. 우리는 Chern-Simons theory를 사용하여 등각적으로 내장된(conformally-embedded) 대칭에 대한 이러한 defect의 일반적인 구성을 제시한다. 이들은 항상 존재하며 어떤 유한한 categorical symmetry에 대한 half-space gauging defect로 실현될 수 있다. 이 기법을 이용하여 우리는 defect를 통한 산란(scattering) 시 fermion이 붙게 되는 twist line, 대응하는 folded boundary state의 boundary central charge, 그리고 그것의 mod-2 값을 갖는 SPT class를 계산한다. 응용으로서 우리는 먼저 abelian symmetry를 고려하고 문헌에 알려진 결과와 일치함을 확인한다. 이어서 우리는 $SU(2) \times SU(4) \times U(1)^2$ 대칭이 보존되는 새로운 예를 연구한다. abelian인 경우와 달리, twist line은 본질적으로 non-invertible하며 산란이 왼쪽으로 향하는지 오른쪽으로 향하는지에 따라 $\text{TY}(\mathbb{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_3)$ 또는 $\text{Rep}(\mathbb{S}_3) \times \mathbb{Z}_3$ 대칭에 속한다.

Abstract

We consider boundary conditions of two-dimensional Dirac fermions which preserve a non-anomalous continuous symmetry which acts differently on the left- and right-movers. Such boundaries are equivalent to topological defects of Weyl fermions preserving the symmetry. We give a general construction of such defects for conformally-embedded symmetries using Chern-Simons theory. They always exist and can be realised as half-space gauging defects for some finite categorical symmetry. Using this technology, we compute the twist lines to which fermions become attached upon scattering through the defect, the boundary central charge of the corresponding folded boundary state, and its mod-2 valued SPT class. As applications, we first consider abelian symmetries and find agreement with known results in the literature. We then study a novel example where an $SU(2) \times SU(4) \times U(1)^2$ symmetry is preserved. In contrast to the abelian case, the twist lines are intrinsically non-invertible and belong to either a $\text{TY}(\mathbb{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_3)$ or $\text{Rep}(\mathbb{S}_3) \times \mathbb{Z}_3$ symmetry depending on whether scattering is to the left or the right.

Finding CFTs from spatial positivity of partition functions

한국어

우리는 modular invariance를 일반화하는 partition function에 대한 positivity 성질을 논의하며, 이는 일반적인 2D CFT에 대해 성립한다고 여기서 추측된다. 우리는 이것이 Rényi entropy의 positivity 성질과 가지는 관계, 그리고 topological defect line의 관점에서 갖는 의미를 보인다. 우리는 이 추측을 적용하여 반드시 modular invariant일 필요는 없는 consistent한 local unitary CFT 모델을 찾는다.

Abstract

We discuss a positivity property for partition functions that generalizes modular invariance and which is here conjectured to hold for general 2D CFTs. We show its relation to positivity properties of Rényi entropies and its meaning in terms of topological defect lines. We apply the conjecture to find consistent local unitary CFT models which are not necessarily modular invariant.

Collinear Gluons from Gravity: Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt Decomposition and Twisted Intersections

arXiv:2610.30235 PDF hep-thgr-qchep-ph
Jin Dong , Stephan Stieberger
한국어

우리는 Einstein-Yang-Mills theory와 그 string 보정에서 one-graviton amplitude로부터 subleading collinear Yang--Mills amplitude를 명시적으로 재구성한다. 우리는 혼합된 open-closed string 관계에서의 monodromy kernel을 gluon label의 word에 대한 가중 Lie-algebraic recursion으로 실현한다. 차수 $\alpha'^r$에서 그 결과물은 많아야 $r$개의 Lie factor를 포함한다. Poincaré--Birkhoff--Witt (PBW) 정리는 gluon 순서가 펼치는 공간을 unsigned Stirling number of the first kind로 주어지는 차원을 갖는 섹터들로 분해한다. one-graviton amplitude의 적절한 shuffle sum은 차수 $r$ 섹터를 추출하며, 이에 대해 string-period matrix는 사라진다. 이에 따라 우리는 재구성 계수가 bipartition factor의 곱에 대한 선형 kinematic factor로 인수분해됨을 보인다. 마지막으로 우리는 재구성 계수를 Parke--Taylor form과, logarithmic four-point differential로부터 재귀적으로 구성된 명시적 forest cocycle 사이의 twisted intersection number로 표현한다.

Abstract

We give an explicit reconstruction of subleading collinear Yang--Mills amplitudes from one-graviton amplitudes in Einstein--Yang--Mills theory and its string corrections. We realize the monodromy kernel in the mixed open-closed string relation as a weighted Lie-algebraic recursion on words of gluon labels. At order $\alpha'^r$, its output contains at most $r$ Lie factors. The Poincaré--Birkhoff--Witt (PBW) theorem decomposes the space spanned by gluon orderings into sectors whose dimensions are given by unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind. Appropriate shuffle sums of one-graviton amplitudes extract the degree-$r$ sector, for which the string-period matrix drops out. Accordingly, we show that the reconstruction coefficients factor into linear kinematic factors over the product of bipartition factors. Finally, we express the reconstruction coefficients as twisted intersection numbers between Parke--Taylor forms and explicit forest cocycles constructed recursively from logarithmic four-point differentials.

Reentrant $d=2+1$ Superfluidity at High Temperature

arXiv:2610.30236 PDF hep-thcond-mat.stat-mechcond-mat.str-el
Subham Dutta Chowdhury , Joan Elias Miro
한국어

우리는 $d=2+1$ 차원에서 global $U(1)$ 대칭을 가지며 임의로 높은 온도에서도 Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless (BKT) ordered phase를 나타내는 UV-complete 모델을 제시한다. 스케일의 신중한 분리는 2차원 Euclidean effective field theory를 산출하며, 이 이론의 구성은 그 soft mode가 강하게 결합하게 되는 경우에도 섭동적으로 제어 가능한 상태로 유지된다. 우리는 이 이론을 기존의 lattice Monte Carlo 결과와 신중하게 맞춤으로써 BKT phase boundary를 결정한다. 이 고온 상(phase)은 $T\to\infty$ 에서도 gapless한 static mode와 대수적으로 감쇠하는 상관관계(correlation)를 유지한다.

Abstract

We present a UV-complete model with a global $U(1)$ symmetry in $d=2+1$ dimensions that exhibits a Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless (BKT) ordered phase at arbitrarily high temperatures. A careful separation of scales yields a two-dimensional Euclidean effective field theory whose construction remains perturbatively controlled even when its soft modes become strongly coupled. We determine the BKT phase boundary by carefully matching this theory to existing lattice Monte Carlo results. The high-temperature phase supports gapless static modes and algebraically decaying correlations even as $T\to\infty$.

Defect flows from functional relations in the asymptotic semiring

arXiv:2610.30242 PDF hep-thcond-mat.str-elmath-ph
Federico Ambrosino , Anatoly Konechny , Ingo Runkel , Gérard M. T. Watts
한국어

우리는 2차원 conformal field theory에서 relevant holomorphic defect field에 의한 topological defect의 섭동을 연구한다. 이는 translation invariant defect를 만들어내며, 이들은 반드시 적외선(infrared)에서 topological defect로 흐른다(flow). Translation invariant defect는 topological defect의 fusion category를 확장하는 monoidal category를 이룬다. 우리는 renormalisation group flow가 translation invariant defect의 semiring으로부터, topological defect로부터 구성하는 "asymptotic semiring"으로 가는 semiring homomorphism을 정의한다고 주장한다. 이 semiring homomorphism은 functional relation을 보존하며, 우리는 Virasoro minimal model의 예를 통해 asymptotic semiring에서의 결과적인 관계가 defect flow의 종착점을 제약함을 보인다. 동반 논문 [1]은 여러 minimal model에서의 이러한 defect flow에 대한 상세한 연구를 수치적으로 그리고 해석적으로 담고 있다.

Abstract

We study perturbations of topological defects by a relevant holomorphic defect field in two-dimensional conformal field theory. This produces translation invariant defects and these necessarily flow to topological defects in the infrared. Translation invariant defects form a monoidal category which extends the fusion category of topological defects. We argue that the renormalisation group flow defines a semiring homomorphism from the semiring of translation invariant defects to an "asymptotic semiring" which we construct from topological defects. The semiring homomorphism preserves functional relations, and we illustrate in the example of Virasoro minimal models that the resulting relations in the asymptotic semiring constrain the defect flow endpoints. The companion paper [1] contains a detailed study of such defect flows in several minimal models, both numerically and analytically.

From bulk flows to boundary generators and costs

arXiv:2610.30246 PDF hep-thgr-qc
Rafael Carrasco , Carlos Pérez-Pardavila
한국어

Holographic complexity는 다양한 bulk observable을 허용하지만, 이들과 boundary state-preparation cost 사이의 관계는 아직 명확하지 않다. 우리는 적절한 bulk observable을 Euclidean source에 의해 준비된 state들의 family 상에서의 quadratic cost geometry와 연관짓는 local한 처방(prescription)을 제안한다. bulk Hamiltonian flow를 Euclidean preparation source로 holographic하게 수송(transport)하는 것을 기반으로, 우리는 정상(stationary) 기준 state에서 수송된 generator의 Hessian을 사용하여 후보 cost metric을 정의한다. 이 Hessian이 positive definite일 때, 최소화된 preparation energy는 bulk observable의 변화를 quadratic order까지 재현한다. 우리는 그 결과로 얻어지는 preparation penalty를 Fubini-Study metric에 대해 상대적으로 특성화하고, AdS$_3$의 scalar perturbation에 대한 maximal volume에 대해 이를 계산한다. large-conformal dimension에서 우리는 cost와 Fubini-Study metric 사이의 잘 알려진 비례관계를 재현한다. 유한한 dimension에서는, bulk field와 그 conjugate momentum을 변위시키는 source variation에 대해 양의 값을 갖지만 일반적으로 서로 다른 penalty를 얻으며, 이는 단일 scalar operator에 대해서도 이 비례관계를 깨뜨린다. 우리는 나아가 large-dimension 영역에서 quadratic scalar 항과 gradient 항에 의한 volume의 deformation을 연구하여, 결합상수(coupling)들이 penalty를 어떻게 수정하고 positivity를 어떻게 제약하는지를 결정한다. 이러한 추가 항들은 field-displacement cost를 선택적으로 변화시키며, gradient 항은 mode 의존성을 도입한다. 우리의 결과는 bulk observable의 선택과 boundary preparation penalty의 할당 사이의 명시적인 local 연결을 제공한다.

Abstract

Holographic complexity admits a broad family of bulk observables, yet their relation to boundary state-preparation costs remains unclear. We propose a local prescription that associates suitable bulk observables with quadratic cost geometries on families of states prepared by Euclidean sources. Building on the holographic transport of bulk Hamiltonian flows to Euclidean preparation sources, we use the Hessian of the transported generator at a stationary reference state to define a candidate cost metric. When this Hessian is positive definite, the minimised preparation energy reproduces the change in the bulk observable to quadratic order. We characterise the resulting preparation penalties relative to the Fubini-Study metric and compute them for the maximal volume in scalar perturbations of AdS$_3$. At large-conformal dimension, we recover the known proportionality between cost and Fubini-Study metrics. At finite dimension, we obtain positive but generally unequal penalties for source variations that displace the bulk field and its conjugate momentum, breaking this proportionality even for a single scalar operator. We further study a deformation of volume by quadratic scalar and gradient terms in the large-dimension regime, determining how the couplings modify the penalties and constrain positivity. These additions selectively change field-displacement costs, with the gradient term introducing mode dependence. Our results provide an explicit local connection between the choice of bulk observables and the assignment of boundary preparation penalties.

3D de Sitter from complex Virasoro TQFT: Observers and knots

arXiv:2610.30247 PDF hep-thmath-ph
Jeevan Chandra , Diego Liska , Boris Post
한국어

Abstract

The Euclidean saddles of 3D Einstein gravity with positive cosmological constant are spherical three-manifolds. When one adds a heavy observer, the backreacted saddles are spherical cone manifolds, with a conical singularity along the observer worldline. We propose exact gravitational partition functions at fixed topology on a class of spherical cone manifolds that undergo hyperbolic-to-spherical transitions as the cone angles increase. Our construction relies on a careful analytic continuation of the product of two Virasoro TQFT partition functions. We test the proposal by showing that the exact partition functions exponentiate in the semiclassical limit to the spherical volume, in agreement with the gravitational on-shell action. This leads to a spherical volume conjecture for the product of continued Virasoro TQFT amplitudes. As further evidence, we relate the theta and tetrahedron graph amplitudes to the two-point function and out-of-time-ordered four-point function of operators on a massive observer's worldline. In appropriate limits, we recover the static patch scalar two-point function with finite observer backreaction and the semiclassical effects of observer recoil. In addition, we analyze examples with knotted worldlines and reproduce their spherical volumes. We also apply our prescription to Seifert manifolds, which are off-shell in AdS$_3$ gravity but admit dS$_3$ saddles, and find agreement with their spherical volumes semiclassically. Finally, using Dehn surgery on torus knots and links, we obtain partition functions on smooth spherical three-manifolds.

Big Crunching Your Big Bang: Doubly Warped Time-Dependent Type II Flux Backgrounds and Scale Separation

Abstract

We construct three analytic, doubly warped cosmological solutions of massless type IIA supergravity with different choices of fluxes, namely, $H_3$, $\tilde F_2$ and $\tilde F_4$ along internal directions. Finally, we analyse the supersymmetry of these backgrounds, which demonstrates that all three solutions break all forms of type IIA supersymmetry.

Pressure in Wilsonian effective field theory

Abstract

Abstract

We then show that there exists a decoupling limit of a timelike ambient fluid which gives rise to an autonomous Carrollian fluid with spontaneously broken Carroll boosts and no source terms. This decoupling limit corresponds to an infinite boost while keeping the enthalpy finite and nonzero, which leads to a divergent ambient energy-momentum tensor. Nevertheless, the induced Carrollian responses do not see the divergent term and remain finite, and an independent Carroll boost Goldstone emerges from a subleading component of the ambient fluid velocity in this limit.
