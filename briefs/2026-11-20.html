Coupling of TQFTs to QFTs and SymTFT interfaces from bordisms

arXiv:2611.25012 PDF hep-th
Max Hübner , Ling Lin , Daniel Robbins , Subham Roy
한국어

M-theory local model은 torsional cycle과 같은 비자명한 topology를 나타낼 수 있으며, 이는 특이점에 국소화된 quantum field theory sector와 local model의 점근 영역을 연결한다. 우리는 이러한 구조가 표준적인 decoupling limit에서 국소화된 QFT와 비자명하게 결합할 수 있는 추가적인 topological sector를 만들어냄을 보인다. 대칭 topological field theory(SymTFT)의 관점에서 보면, 이러한 local model과 관련된 물리적 경계조건이 수정된다. 우리는 국소 특이점과 점근 경계 사이를 보간하는 기하학적 bordism으로부터 SymTFT interface를 구성하는 방법을 제시한다. 이 구성을 frozen $D_5$ 특이점의 circle compactification에 적용하여, 전체 이론의 $\mathbb{Z}_4$ SymTFT와 국소 $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ gauge sector의 $\mathbb{Z}_2$ SymTFT 사이의 topological interface를 규명한다. 또한 gauge theory와 추가적인 topological quantum field theory 사이의 결과적인 결합이 frozen theory의 electric symmetry와 magnetic symmetry 사이에서 나타나는 명백한 불일치를 설명함을 보인다.

Abstract

M-theory local models may exhibit torsional cycles and other non-trivial topology which interpolate between quantum field theory sectors localized at singularities and the asymptotic regions of the local model. We show that such structures give rise, in standard decoupling limits, to additional topological sectors which can couple non-trivially to the localized QFT. From a symmetry topological field theory (SymTFT) perspective, the physical boundary conditions associated to such local models are modified. We give a construction of SymTFT interfaces from geometric bordisms interpolating between the local singularity and the asymptotic boundary. We apply this construction to the circle compactification of a frozen $D_5$ singularity, identifying a topological interface between the $\mathbb{Z}_4$ SymTFT of the full theory and the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ SymTFT of its local $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ gauge sector. We show that the resulting coupling between the gauge theory and an additional topological quantum field theory accounts for the apparent mismatch between electric and magnetic symmetries of the frozen theory.

Abstract

This review is written with a simple question in mind: what does one need to know to enter the literature on Scherk-Schwarz compactifications? It is organized as a sequence of questions that a graduate student might naturally ask, together with the concepts, calculations, and formal tools needed to work through the literature. The discussion starts at an elementary field-theory level and gradually develops toward more advanced aspects of supergravity, orbifolds, quantum effects, and string theory. Phenomenological applications are deliberately left aside.

Quiver-Invariant Triality between Brane Brick Models

Abstract

We study quiver-invariant triality between 2d (0,2) supersymmetric gauge theories described by brane brick models associated with toric Calabi-Yau 4-folds. The dual theories share the same gauge groups, field content and E-terms but have distinct J-terms. We present an explicit pair of such brane brick models and identify the sequence of triality mutations relating them. This sequence returns the quiver to its original form while transforming the J- and E-terms. We explain the relation between quiver-invariant triality and the orbifold reduction of quiver-invariant brane tilings.

Abstract

We formulate heterotic and type II $\mathbb T^6/\mathbb Z_n$ asymmetric orbifolds preserving all 16 right-moving supersymmetries and leading to 4-dimensional islands without massless moduli other than the dilaton. We establish the consistency of the orbifold conformal field theory, including the validity of operator interpretation. In the heterotic case we classify all islands based on the $\Gamma(22,6)$ lattice constructed via the Leech lattice. In the type II case we also provide a classification of allowed $\Gamma(6,6)$ lattices and orbifold generators. In both cases we further examine whether a particular orbifold admits an archipelago of different islands arising from inequivalent realizations of the orbifold action.

arXiv:2611.25021 PDF hep-th

한국어

Abstract

We consider orbifolds of 4d $\mathcal{N}=4$ super-Yang--Mills theory that preserve minimal supersymmetry and study their conformal manifolds, focusing on two families of Abelian orbifolds in the planar limit. We compute their superconformal indices, construct their complete single-trace BPS spectra and identify the conformal manifolds. These are generically spanned by a subset of the gauge-couplings, whose number we derive from both gauge and string theory, and a universal Leigh--Strassler-type superpotential deformation. We then construct the complete, planar one-loop dilatation operator on these conformal manifolds, giving perturbative access to the full non-BPS spectrum away from the orbifold point. This lays the groundwork for studying special loci of the conformal manifold in a forthcoming Part II.

Stringy Corrections from Gravity: Predictions for BFSS and ABJM

arXiv:2611.25022 PDF hep-th
Aidan Herderschee , Victor Ivo , Henry W. Lin , Yifan Wang
한국어

string theory의 엄청난 발전에도 불구하고, 많은 holographic observable에 대한 leading stringy correction은 여전히 알려져 있지 않다. 그럼에도 String/M-theory duality는 이를 gravity만으로 결정할 수 있게 해준다. 우리는 circle 위의 one-loop 11d supergravity의 winding mode로부터, metric, dilaton, Ramond--Ramond one-form에 대한 type IIA effective action의 leading tree-level stringy term을 Lorentz generator에 대한 유한한 supertrace 형태의 closed form으로 도출한다. thermal D0-brane geometry에서 이는 BFSS matrix model의 저온 thermodynamics에 대한 첫 번째 correction을 제공한다. 그 계수는 발표된 lattice Monte Carlo 추정값과 $\approx 20\%$ 차이가 난다. four-form flux를 포함하면, 동일한 방법으로 localization으로 얻어진 three-sphere 위의 ABJM free energy의 $\zeta(3)k^{2}$ 항을 재현한다.

Abstract

Despite enormous progress in string theory, the leading stringy corrections to many holographic observables remain unknown. String/M-theory dualities nonetheless allow them to be determined from gravity alone. From the winding modes of one-loop 11d supergravity on a circle, we derive the leading tree-level stringy terms in the type IIA effective action for the metric, dilaton, and Ramond--Ramond one-form, in closed form as a finite supertrace over Lorentz generators. On the thermal D0-brane geometry, this gives the first correction to the low-temperature thermodynamics of the BFSS matrix model. Its coefficient differs from published lattice Monte Carlo estimates by $\approx 20\%$. With the four-form flux included, the same method reproduces the $\zeta(3)k^{2}$ term of the ABJM free energy on the three-sphere obtained by localization.

Actionless Branes on Axion Wormholes

arXiv:2611.25026 PDF hep-thgr-qc
A. Ramesh Chandra
한국어

Euclidean axion wormhole이 모든 instanton이 여전히 억제된 채로 무한대까지 뻗어 나갈 수 있는가? imaginary distance bound는 그럴 수 없다고 말한다. 즉, 어떤 instanton은 먼저 actionless가 되어야 한다. Calabi-Yau compactification은 이 bound를 위반하는 것처럼 보인다. 여러 axion이 field excursion을 나누어 가지며, 그중 어느 하나에 charged된 instanton들은 끝까지 억제된 채로 남기 때문이다. 우리는 wormhole이 이러한 instanton으로부터는 숨을 수 있지만 그 bound state로부터는 숨을 수 없음을 보인다. 정렬되지 않은 central charge는 bound state가 그 구성 요소보다 더 낮은 비용을 갖게 하는 반면, axion은 모든 구성 요소에 작용하기 때문이다. Neveu-Schwarz two-form의 두 axion이 지지하는 type IIB wormhole에서, string charge를 띤 fivebrane은 axion의 상대적 부호가 어느 쪽이든, 그리고 axion들이 excursion을 어떻게 나누든, large volume에서 무한대까지 가는 거리의 3분의 2 이내에서 actionless가 된다. S-dual 변수에서 이는 한쪽 부호에서는 magnetic flux를 가진 D5-brane이고, 다른 쪽 부호에서는 D5-NS5 bound state이다. axion을 더 추가해도 도움이 되지 않는다. 중요한 것은 axion의 개수가 아니라 saxion sector의 homogeneity 차수이기 때문에, 동일한 brane이 모든 Calabi-Yau threefold에서 actionless가 된다. 이러한 bound state는 convex-hull axion weak gravity conjecture가 요구하는 것이다.

Abstract

Can a Euclidean axion wormhole extend to infinity with every instanton still suppressed? The imaginary distance bound says it cannot: some instanton must become actionless first. Calabi-Yau compactifications appear to violate the bound: several axions share the field excursion, and the instantons charged under any one of them stay suppressed all the way. We show that the wormhole can hide from these instantons but not from their bound states: misaligned central charges make a bound state cheaper than its constituents, while the axions act on every constituent. On the type IIB wormhole supported by two axions of the Neveu-Schwarz two-form, a fivebrane carrying string charge becomes actionless at large volume within two thirds of the way to infinity, for either relative sign of the axions and however they share the excursion. In S-dual variables it is a D5-brane with magnetic flux for one sign and a D5-NS5 bound state for the other. Adding more axions does not help: the same branes become actionless on every Calabi-Yau threefold, because what counts is the degree of homogeneity of the saxion sector, not the number of axions. These bound states are what the convex-hull axion weak gravity conjecture requires.

Twist and Shout: defect lines on the Rényi torus

arXiv:2611.25032 PDF hep-th
Naveen Balaji Umasankar
한국어

우리는 critical system의 두 영역 사이의 entanglement가 non-invertible symmetry를 포함한 그 symmetry에 대해 무엇을 드러낼 수 있는지 고찰한다. 2차원 conformal field theory의 두 구간에 대해 second Rényi entropy는 torus 위의 partition function이며, topological defect line을 구간 중 하나에 감을 수 있다. on-site symmetry의 경우 이는 reduced density matrix와 한 구간에 작용하는 symmetry 하에서의 그 image 사이의 overlap을 계산하며, 일반적으로는 cross-ratio에만 의존하는 observable을 정의한다. 구간들이 서로 가까워질 때 이 observable은 line이 끝날 수 있는 가장 가벼운 operator가 정하는 비율로 소멸하며, 그 monodromy는 그러한 operator들의 spin에 의해 고정된다. 이 spin들이 fusion category에 의해 제약되므로, 이 observable은 't Hooft anomaly를 검출하고 동일한 fusion rule을 갖는 category들을 구별한다. 이는 $(E_8)_1$ theory의 $\mathbb{Z}_2$, $\mathbb{Z}_3$ 및 Fibonacci line과, Ising model의 spin-flip 및 Kramers-Wannier line에 대해 closed form으로 계산할 수 있으며, spin-flip 결과는 critical Ising chain에서의 exact computation과 일치한다. symmetry에 대해 평균을 취하면 이 observable은 mutual information에 대한 알려진 Jones-index bound로 환원된다. 또한 관련된 twist-field crossing equation이 modular bootstrap의 한 형태임을 보이고, 더 높은 genus의 surface 위의 entanglement로부터 전체 fusion category를 복원할 수 있는지를 논의한다.

Abstract

We consider what the entanglement between two regions of a critical system can reveal about its symmetries, including non-invertible ones. For two intervals in a two-dimensional conformal field theory, the second Rényi entropy is a partition function on a torus, and a topological defect line can be wrapped around one of the intervals. For an on-site symmetry this computes the overlap of the reduced density matrix with its image under the symmetry acting on one interval, and in general it defines an observable that depends only on the cross-ratio. As the intervals approach each other, the observable vanishes at a rate set by the lightest operator on which the line can end, and its monodromy is fixed by the spins of such operators. Since these spins are constrained by the fusion category, the observable detects 't Hooft anomalies and distinguishes categories with the same fusion rules. It can be computed in closed form for a $\mathbb{Z}_2$, a $\mathbb{Z}_3$ and a Fibonacci line of the $(E_8)_1$ theory, and for the spin-flip and Kramers-Wannier lines of the Ising model, where the spin-flip result agrees with an exact computation in the critical Ising chain. Averaged over a symmetry, the observable reduces to a known Jones-index bound on the mutual information. We also show that the associated twist-field crossing equations are a form of the modular bootstrap, and discuss whether the full fusion category can be recovered from entanglement on surfaces of higher genus.

Entanglement entropy from fundamental strings

Houwen Wu , Shuxuan Ying
한국어

이전 연구에서 우리는 off-shell string theory로부터 local entropy capacity를 유도하고, 이를 gravitational replica construction 및 flow-cut duality와 결합하여 physical fundamental string이 운반하는 entropy flow를 normalize하였다. 본 논문에서는 이 틀을 holographic CFT$_2$의 interval entanglement에 적용한다. Abstract

In previous work, we derived the local entropy capacity from off-shell string theory and combined it with the gravitational replica construction and flow-cut duality to normalize the entropy flow carried by physical fundamental strings. In this paper, we apply this framework to interval entanglement in holographic CFT$_2$. We construct explicit on-shell F1 worldsheets in the AdS$_3$ vacuum and planar BTZ, derive their Kalb-Ramond source currents, and show that the normalized spatial currents saturate the corresponding RT surface. Their flux reproduces both the vacuum single interval entropy and the finite temperature CFT$_2$ result. We then vary the NS-NS flux fraction. Although the total maximal flux remains unchanged, the F1 equations continuously redistribute the strings among different parts of the complement. In planar BTZ, the resulting signed flux through the bifurcation line reproduces the extensive thermal entropy and can vanish in the pure NS-NS limit without changing the interval entropy. Thus the on-shell string dynamics additionally determines its physical routing and boundary contact structure.

$C_T$ and $C_J$ of general scalar--fermion conformal field theories in $d=4-ε$

arXiv:2611.25055 PDF hep-thcond-mat.str-el
Shiroman Prakash , Shubham Kumar Sinha
한국어

우리는 $d=4-\epsilon$에서 scalar와 fermion의 일반적인 renormalizable theory의 임의의 fixed point에서 stress-tensor와 conserved-current two-point function의 계수인 $C_T$와 $C_J$를 order $\epsilon^3$까지 계산한다. chiral Ising, XY, Heisenberg model과 $d=3$에서 emergent supersymmetry를 갖는 fixed point에 대한 명시적 결과를 제시한다. 4차원 대응물이 없는 theory에서는 Yukawa vertex가 홀수 개인 fermion loop가 order $\epsilon^3$에서 기여하는데, 이는 $d=3$으로부터 벗어난 continuation이 모호한 trace를 통해 나타난다. 우리는 이를 $\mathcal N=1$ supersymmetry로 고정하며, supercurrent와 flavor current의 fermionic partner의 two-point function을 four loop까지 계산한다. $d=3$에서 Padé approximant는 $\mathcal N=2$ super-Ising model의 exact $C_T$를 매우 가깝게 재현한다. graphene의 charge-density-wave, Kekulé, Néel transition에서의 universal conductivity를 $\sigma/\sigma_{\rm free}\approx0.91$, $0.79$--$0.83$, $0.64$--$0.74$로 추정하고, topological insulator 표면의 supersymmetric transition에서의 exact conductivity가 $\sigma/\sigma_{\rm free}=\frac{9}{10}\,C_T/C_{T,\rm free}\approx0.654$이며, 이는 이전에 보고된 값보다 $10\%$ 낮음을 보인다.

Abstract

We compute $C_T$ and $C_J$, the coefficients of the stress-tensor and conserved-current two-point functions, to order $\epsilon^3$ at any fixed point of the general renormalizable theory of scalars and fermions in $d=4-\epsilon$. We give explicit results for the chiral Ising, XY and Heisenberg models and for fixed points with emergent supersymmetry in $d=3$. In $d=3$, Padé approximants closely reproduce the exact $C_T$ of the $\mathcal N=2$ super-Ising model. We estimate the universal conductivity at the charge-density-wave, Kekulé and Néel transitions of graphene to be $\sigma/\sigma_{\rm free}\approx0.91$, $0.79$--$0.83$ and $0.64$--$0.74$, and show that the exact conductivity at the supersymmetric transition on the surface of a topological insulator is $\sigma/\sigma_{\rm free}=\frac{9}{10}\,C_T/C_{T,\rm free}\approx0.654$, $10\%$ below the value previously reported.
