Feynman integrals, coactions and Lie-algebra representations

arXiv:2611.26679 PDF hep-thmath.AGmath.NT
Emiel Claasen , Mehregan Doroudiani , Claude Duhr , Axel Kleinschmidt , Franca Lippert , Oliver Schlotterer , Yoann Sohnle
한국어

Feynman integral의 canonical differential equation과 특정 Lie algebra 값을 갖는 flat connection의 행렬 표현 사이의 연관성을 제시한다. string theory의 통찰과 이러한 Lie algebra에 연관된 period의 성질을 활용하여, differential equation의 single-valued solution과 integral의 coaction을 도출한다. Lie algebra의 representation theory와 연결되며 통제된 방식으로 $\varepsilon$-factorisation에서 벗어날 수 있는 새로운 형태의 differential equation을 도입한다. 관련된 예제들을 통해 이 형식론을 설명한다.

Abstract

We outline a link between canonical differential equations of Feynman integrals and matrix representations of flat connections taking values in certain Lie algebras. Leveraging insights from string theory and properties of periods associated with these Lie algebras, we deduce single-valued solutions to the differential equations and coactions for the integrals. We introduce a novel form of the differential equations which connects with the representation theory of the Lie algebras and may depart from $\varepsilon$-factorisation in a controlled way. We illustrate the formalism through relevant examples.

A matrix model of an M5--brane on a plane--wave background

arXiv:2611.26687 PDF hep-th
Carmine Montella
한국어

최근 제안된 $\mathcal{A}_6$ matrix model의 plane-wave background 상에서의 maximally supersymmetric deformation을 구성한다. 또한 이 background에서 five-sphere 위의 light-cone M5-brane을 chiral two-form과 fermion의 동역학을 포함하여 최저 harmonics로 reduction함으로써 동일한 이론을 독립적으로 유도한다. 이어서 이 모형의 모든 classical vacua를 분류한다. 그중 하나가 fivebrane giant graviton임을 보이고, 그 fluctuation이 단일 $\mathfrak{su}(2|4)$ multiplet을 이루며, 이것이 BMN theory의 large $N$ limit에서 확인된 spherical transverse fivebrane의 $\ell=1$ 준위를 재현함을 보인다. 더 나아가 dynamical tensor는 flux가 brane의 크기와 균형을 이루는 새로운 vacua의 족을 지지하며, 그 spectrum은 fuzzy membrane의 spectrum을 포함한다. 마지막으로, BLG theory가 이 모형 안에 존재함을 보인다. 이는 massless theory의 Coulomb branch 상에서도, 그리고 두 membrane이 fivebrane으로 polarized된 massive theory의 giant graviton 주변에서도 성립한다.

Abstract

We construct the maximally supersymmetric deformation of the recently proposed $\mathcal{A}_6$ matrix model on a plane--wave background. We derive the same theory independently, by reducing the light--cone M5--brane on a five--sphere in this background to its lowest harmonics, including the dynamics of its chiral two--form and of its fermions. We then classify all its classical vacua. We show that one of them is the fivebrane giant graviton, and that its fluctuations form a single $\mathfrak{su}(2|4)$ multiplet, which reproduces the $\ell=1$ level of the spherical transverse fivebrane identified in the large $N$ limit of BMN theory. Moreover, the dynamical tensor supports new families of vacua, where the flux balances the size of the brane, and whose spectrum contains that of a fuzzy membrane. Finally, we show that BLG theory sits inside the model, both on a Coulomb branch of the massless theory and around the giant graviton of the massive one, where the two membranes are polarized into the fivebrane.

Onset of quantum chaos in a family of two-dimensional conformal field theories

arXiv:2611.26694 PDF hep-thnlin.CD
Felix M. Haehl , Moshe Rozali
한국어

2차원 conformal field theory의 한 족에서 non-BPS primary operator의 scaling dimension에 random matrix statistics가 나타남을 보인다. 구체적으로, Kummer orbifold point $\mathbb T^4/\mathbb Z_2$ 근방에서 K3 target space와 $\mathcal N=(4,4)$ supersymmetry를 갖는 sigma model을 고려한다. free-fermion radius에서 $\mathbb Z_2^4$-invariant sector의 spin-zero spectrum은 정수 에너지에서 정확히 degenerate하다. 이 대칭을 보존하는 exactly marginal blow-up deformation은 4차원 공간을 이루며, 그 방향을 unit quaternion으로 매개화한다. 각 deformation 방향을 따라 degenerate한 준위들의 1차 splitting, 즉 coupling이 0으로 갈 때의 leading anomalous dimension을 계산한다. 짝수 준위의 hidden $\mathbb Z_2$를 포함하여 모든 exact symmetry가 분해된 sector에서, 일반적인 방향을 따른 0이 아닌 1차 shift는 Gaussian orthogonal ensemble (GOE)을 따르며, 이는 여러 spectral diagnostic을 계산하여 검증한다. 이를 enhanced symmetry를 갖는 잘 알려진 singlet deformation과 정량적으로 비교한다. 알려진 모든 대칭을 분해한 후에도 singlet deformation의 1차 spectrum은 정확한 degeneracy를 가지며, gap ratio는 에너지가 증가함에 따라 GOE 값에 천천히 접근할 뿐이고, unfolded spectrum은 GOE의 long-range rigidity를 결여한다.

Abstract

We exhibit random matrix statistics in the scaling dimensions of non-BPS primary operators in a family of two-dimensional conformal field theories. Specifically, we consider the sigma model with K3 target space and $\mathcal N=(4,4)$ supersymmetry in a neighborhood of the Kummer orbifold point $\mathbb T^4/\mathbb Z_2$. At the free-fermion radius, the spin-zero spectrum of the $\mathbb Z_2^4$-invariant sector is exactly degenerate at integer energies. The exactly marginal blow-up deformations that preserve this symmetry form a four-dimensional space, whose directions we parametrize by unit quaternions. Along each deformation direction, we compute the first-order splitting of the degenerate levels, i.e., the leading anomalous dimensions as the coupling goes to zero. In sectors where all exact symmetries are resolved, including a hidden $\mathbb Z_2$ at the even levels, the non-zero first-order shifts along a generic direction follow the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble (GOE), as we verify by computing several spectral diagnostics. We compare quantitatively with the well-known singlet deformation, which has enhanced symmetry: even after all its known symmetries are resolved, its first-order spectrum has exact degeneracies, its gap ratio approaches the GOE value only slowly as the energy increases, and its unfolded spectrum lacks the long-range rigidity of the GOE.

Energy and Charge Transmission at Conformal Interfaces: Relations and Bounds

arXiv:2611.27002 PDF hep-thcond-mat.str-el
Yuma Furuta , Yuya Kusuki , Toshiki Onagi , Yin Tang
한국어

대칭은 에너지와 전하가 conformal interface를 얼마나 독립적으로 통과할 수 있는지를 제약한다. diagonal affine algebra를 보존하는 Wess--Zumino--Witten (WZW) model $G_{k_L}$과 $G_{k_R}$ 사이의 interface에 대해, 에너지 및 전하 transmission coefficient 사이의 정확한 관계 $c_{LR}=2(\dim G)k_{LR}/(k_L+k_R+2h^\vee)$를 유도한다. outgoing energy의 positivity와 함께, 이 관계는 level이 서로 다를 때 완전한 에너지 transmission을 배제한다. 추가적인 bulk degrees of freedom가 있으면 등식은 coset central charge로 제어되는 bound로 대체된다. 그러면 더 낮은 level의 WZW model로부터의 완전 transmission은 반대편의 coset central charge에 하한을 부과한다. linear response에서 이 관계는 열전도도와 전하 전도도의 비를 interface와 무관하게 두 bulk 비의 harmonic mean으로 고정하며, coset sector는 고정된 전하 전도도에서 열전도도에 bound를 준다. 이 interface와 무관한 비는 WZW model 사이의 conformal interface에 대한 일반화된 Wiedemann--Franz law이다. 전하 transmission coefficient $k_{LR}$은 또한 equilibrium charge fluctuation을 지배하며, boundary-condition-changing operator의 scaling dimension이 회전각에 대해 갖는 leading 이차 의존성을 고정한다.

Abstract

Symmetry constrains how independently energy and charge can pass through a conformal interface. For interfaces between Wess--Zumino--Witten (WZW) models $G_{k_L}$ and $G_{k_R}$ preserving the diagonal affine algebra, we derive an exact relation between the energy and charge transmission coefficients, $c_{LR}=2(\dim G)k_{LR}/(k_L+k_R+2h^\vee)$. Together with positivity of outgoing energy, this relation excludes complete energy transmission at unequal levels. With additional bulk degrees of freedom, the equality is replaced by bounds controlled by the coset central charges. Complete transmission from a lower-level WZW model then imposes a lower bound on the coset central charge on the other side. In linear response, the relation fixes the ratio of thermal to charge conductance to the harmonic mean of the two bulk ratios, independently of the interface, while coset sectors bound the thermal conductance at fixed charge conductance. This interface-independent ratio is a generalized Wiedemann--Franz law for conformal interfaces between WZW models. The charge transmission coefficient $k_{LR}$ also governs equilibrium charge fluctuations and fixes the leading quadratic dependence of the boundary-condition-changing operator's scaling dimension on the rotation angle.

Perturbative $S$-matrix Unitarity in Higher-Derivative Theory of Fermions

arXiv:2611.27021 PDF hep-th
Yugo Abe , Takeo Inami , Keisuke Izumi , Shinsuke Kawai
한국어

four-Fermi interaction을 갖는 higher-derivative fermion theory를 구성한다. quantum field theory에서 tree-level scattering amplitude의 좋은 high-energy behavior가 renormalizability를 함의한다는 것이 추측되어 있으며, 이를 tree-unitarity criterion이라 부른다. higher-derivative fermion theory가 이 criterion을 만족하는지 조사하기 위해 $S$-matrix의 구조를 자세히 살펴본다. higher-derivative kinetic term 때문에 spinor field의 mass dimension이 통상적인 fermion과 달라지며, 그 결과 ghost state를 도입하는 대가로 four-Fermi interaction이 renormalizable해진다. bosonic ghost와 달리 fermionic ghost는 에너지 고유값의 부호와 무관하게 필연적으로 negative-norm state를 낳음을 보인다. 나아가 통상적인 four-Fermi theory와 대조적으로 higher-derivative four-Fermi theory는 perturbative $S$-matrix unitarity를 만족함을 보인다. tree-unitarity criterion에 비추어, 이러한 결과는 higher-derivative fermion theory의 renormalizability를 뒷받침하는 증거를 제공하며, higher-derivative scalar theory에 대한 우리의 이전 결과를 fermionic한 경우로 일반화한다.

Abstract

We construct a higher-derivative fermion theory with four-Fermi interactions. In quantum field theory, it is conjectured that good high-energy behavior of tree-level scattering amplitudes implies renormalizability, a notion referred to as the tree-unitarity criterion. To investigate whether this criterion is satisfied in a higher-derivative fermion theory, we examine the structure of the $S$-matrix in detail. Owing to the higher-derivative kinetic term, the mass dimension of the spinor field differs from that of a conventional fermion, rendering four-Fermi interactions renormalizable at the cost of introducing ghost states. We show that, unlike bosonic ghosts, fermionic ghosts inevitably give rise to negative-norm states regardless of the sign of their energy eigenvalues. We further demonstrate that, in contrast to conventional four-Fermi theories, the higher-derivative four-Fermi theory satisfies perturbative $S$-matrix unitarity. In light of the tree-unitarity criterion, these results provide supporting evidence for the renormalizability of higher-derivative fermion theories and generalize our previous findings on higher-derivative scalar theories to the fermionic case.

arXiv:2611.27134 PDF hep-thhep-lat
Ross Dempsey , Anna-Maria E. Glück , Silviu S. Pufu , Benjamin T. Søgaard
한국어

본 논문에서는 tensor network를 이용하여 $(1+1)$차원 gauge theory의 soliton을 연구하는 틀을 개발하고, 이를 massive Schwinger model에서의 $e^+e^-$ 충돌 시뮬레이션에 적용한다. $\theta = \pi$이고 bare fermion mass가 임계값을 넘는 영역에서 작업한다. 이 영역에는 두 개의 degenerate vacua가 있으며, $e^+$와 $e^-$ 입자는 두 vacua 사이를 interpolate하는 soliton state로 구현된다. infinite tensor network 방법을 사용하여 이러한 soliton state를 찾고 특성화하는 방법을 상세히 설명한 뒤, 그 충돌의 real-time simulation을 제시한다. 이러한 충돌에서는 에너지가 큰 flux string이 형성되며, 최종 상태가 elastic 궤적과 string-breaking 궤적의 중첩임을 보인다.

Abstract

In this paper, we develop a framework for studying solitons in $(1+1)$-dimensional gauge theories using tensor networks, and we apply it to simulate $e^+e^-$ collisions in the massive Schwinger model. We work at $\theta = \pi$ and at a bare fermion mass exceeding the critical value; in this regime, there are two degenerate vacua, and the $e^+$ and $e^-$ particles are realized as soliton states interpolating between them. We carefully explain how to find and characterize such soliton states using infinite tensor network methods, and then exhibit real-time simulations of their collisions. These collisions involve the formation of an energetic flux string, and we demonstrate that our final states are superpositions of elastic and string-breaking trajectories.

Abstract

We also conjecture a series of underlying (unexplored) modular invariants, connecting the conformal embedding and the pairing.

한국어

KAN은 여전히 parameter 효율이 더 높고 고정된 epoch 예산에서 더 robust하며, 조정된 activation function을 사용한 MLP는 고정된 wall-clock time에서 적어도 동등한 성능을 보인다. 또한 연속적인 gradient descent는 우리의 neural field의 topological sector를 매끄럽게 바꿀 수 없음을 논증하여, neural variational solver에서 관찰된 topological freezing의 기원을 규명한다. Skyrmion에 적용한 neural method에 대한 우리의 결과는 고전적 topological soliton에 대한 AI-agentic benchmark, 나아가 AI-assisted soliton physics를 위한 연구 기반을 마련한다.

Abstract

We propose the three-dimensional Skyrme model as a topological benchmark for neural variational methods and construct fully three-dimensional Skyrmions with baryon number $B=1,\ldots,4$ without imposing a symmetry ansatz, with high precision. For $B=2,3,4$, the relaxation of these Skyrmions starts from $B$ unit-charge lumps with random positions and orientations, which carry no symmetry information. The known toroidal, tetrahedral, and cubic Skyrmions nevertheless emerge spontaneously. The resulting variational energies for $B=1,\ldots,4$ lie slightly below the published reference values. The neural method highlights the high accuracy for retaining the topological charge, differing from $B$ by at most $10^{-6}$ in the highest-precision solutions. Our numerical method goes through physical constraint tests such as the Derrick virial relation and the Faddeev--Bogomolny bound, together with evaluation / verification through independent quadratures and resolution sweeps. We then compare Kolmogorov--Arnold networks (KANs) with multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) in more than a thousand training runs, both with and without a rational-map ansatz. With default settings, KANs appear markedly superior, but most of the gap disappears once the MLP activation function and learning rate are tuned. KANs remain more parameter-efficient and more robust at fixed epoch budget, while MLPs with the tuned activation function perform at least as well at fixed wall-clock time. We also argue that continuous gradient descent cannot smoothly change the topological sector of our neural field, clarifying the origin of topological freezing observed in our neural variational solvers. Our results on the neural methods applied to Skyrmions pave a research ground for AI-agentic benchmarks for classical topological solitons, toward AI-assisted soliton physics.

The $a$-function in fractional dimensions
